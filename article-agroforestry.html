Agroforestry — Geedaha iyo BeeristaAgroforestry — Trees on the Farmالزراعة الحرجيةKilimo cha Misitu Shambani

2026-05-258 daqiiqo8 min read8 دقائقdakika 8

Agroforestry waa habka loo isku daro geedaha, dalagga, iyo xoolaha hal hektar. Beerta agroforestry leh waxay soo saartaa 1.5–3 jeer dakhli badan oo hektarkii ka badan beerta caadiga ah — iyada oo ay yaraysaa khataraha cimilada. Beero Soomaaliyeed oo isticmaalaya gob-anaca, qansax, iyo cambaha waxay si dabiici ah u maamulaan agroforestry. Agroforestry integrates trees, crops, and livestock on the same hectare. An agroforestry plot can earn 1.5–3× more per hectare than monocropping — while cutting climate risk. Somali farms using Acacia, Faidherbia, and mango already practise it naturally. الزراعة الحرجية تدمج الأشجار والمحاصيل والماشية في نفس الهكتار، 1.5–3 أضعاف الدخل. Agroforestry huunganisha miti, mazao, na mifugo. Mapato 1.5–3× zaidi.

Habab Agroforestry ahAgroforestry Systemsأنظمة الزراعة الحرجيةMifumo

1) Alley Cropping1) Alley Cropping1) الزراعة بين الصفوف1) Alley Cropping

Safafka geedaha digirta ah (Leucaena, Gliricidia) waxa la kala dhig dhigaa 5–10 m. Inta u dhaxaysa, beero galley, masaggada, ama khudaar. Geedaha la jaro 2 jeer sannadkii — caleemaha qoyan waxay ku biiriyaan ciidda nitrogen-ka. Rows of N-fixing trees (Leucaena, Gliricidia) 5–10 m apart. Crops grow in the alleys between. Trees are pruned twice a year — green leaves mulch the crop and fertilise the soil. صفوف أشجار البقول كل 5–10 م مع زراعة المحاصيل بينها. Safu za miti kila m 5–10 na mazao kati.

2) Silvopasture (Geedaha + Xoolaha)2) Silvopasture2) أشجار + ماشية2) Miti + Mifugo

Geedaha (Acacia, Faidherbia) ayaa lagu kala dhig dhigaa daaqa xoolaha. Geedaha waxay siiyaan hooska, caleemaha quudin, iyo digirta cuni karta. Lo'da iyo geela waxay ka faa'iidaan hooska, oo wax-soosaarka caanaha wuxuu kordhaa 15–25%. Trees (Acacia, Faidherbia) on grazing land provide shade, edible leaves, and pods. Cattle and camels gain shade — milk yields rise 15–25%. أشجار في المراعي تزيد الحليب 15–25%. Miti malishoni huongeza maziwa 15–25%.

3) Windbreaks (Ka-difaaca Dabaysha)3) Windbreaks3) كاسرات الرياح3) Vipenyo vya Upepo

Saf 2–3 ah oo geedaha dheer ee xudduuda beerta. Geedaha caanka ah: Casuarina equisetifolia, Eucalyptus, Cypress. Saamaynta: ka difaac dhirta 10× sare ah masaafada geedaha — geed 5 m dheer = 50 m difaac. 2–3 rows of tall trees along the field edge. Common species: Casuarina, Eucalyptus, Cypress. A 5 m tree shelters crops up to 50 m downwind. 2–3 صفوف على الحدود، شجرة 5 م تحمي 50 م. Safu 2–3 za miti mirefu kingoni.

4) Home Gardens / Beerta Guriga4) Home Gardens4) حدائق المنزل4) Bustani za Nyumbani

Habka aasaasi ahaan dadka Soomaaliyeed: cambaha, mooska, geedo, khudaar, iyo digaag — dhammaantood hal hektar. Beerta guriga waxay siisaa cunto, dakhli, iyo fasaxa daawasho. The classic Somali compound: mango, banana, herbs, vegetables, and poultry — all on a small plot. Home gardens deliver food, income, and biodiversity. المانجو، الموز، الخضار، والدجاج معاً. Maembe, ndizi, mboga, kuku pamoja.

Sida loo BilaaboHow to Startكيف نبدأJinsi ya Kuanza

1) Bilow 10–20 geed hektarkii (yar). 2) Beero xilliga roobka (gu' iyo deyr) si ay xididada u qoto dheeraadaan. 3) Daboolaad geedaha cusub si xoolaha aysan u cunin (caws ama qodob). 4) Waraabi geedaha 6–12 bilood ee ugu horeysa, ka dib xididadu way iskaa biyaha helayaan. 5) Markaad fahantid, kor u qaad 50–80 geed/hektar. 1) Start with 10–20 trees/ha. 2) Plant at the start of the rains so roots establish. 3) Protect young trees from livestock with brush or fencing. 4) Water for the first 6–12 months, then they're self-sufficient. 5) Scale up to 50–80 trees/ha once you're comfortable. ابدأ بـ10–20 شجرة/هكتار في الموسم المطير.
