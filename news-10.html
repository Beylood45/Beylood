Saadaalka Deyr 2026: Roob Caadi ah Soomaaliya iyo Geeska Afrika Deyr 2026 Forecast: Near-Normal Rains for Somalia & Horn توقعات الديري 2026: أمطار شبه طبيعية للصومال والقرن الأفريقي Utabiri wa Deyr 2026: Mvua za Karibu Kawaida

2026-06-06 5 daqiiqo 5 min 5 دقائق dakika 5

Hay'adda FAO ee Soomaaliya iyo SWALIM (Somalia Water and Land Information Management) ayaa saadaalisay xilliga Deyrta 2026 (Oktoobar–Diseembar) inuu noqon doono mid roob caadi ah ku helayo dhulalka beeraha. Tani waxay yarayn doontaa cabsidii abaaraha kadib markii xilliga Gu' 2026 uu hoos u dhacay 15%. Somalia FAO and SWALIM (Somalia Water and Land Information Management) project the Deyr 2026 season (October–December) will bring near-normal rains across agricultural belts. This eases drought concerns following the 15% below-average Gu 2026. توقعت FAO وSWALIM أمطار الديري 2026 شبه طبيعية — تخفف مخاوف الجفاف بعد جو هطول دون المعدل بـ15٪. FAO na SWALIM zinatabiri Deyr 2026 (Oktoba–Desemba) kuwa karibu na kawaida — punguza wasiwasi wa ukame.

  • Gobolada Bartamaha: 180–250 mm — caadi.Central regions: 180–250 mm — normal.الوسط: 180–250 ملم.Kati: 180–250 mm.
  • Gobolada Waqooyi (Somaliland, Puntland): 100–180 mm — caadi ka hoos 10%.Northern regions (Somaliland, Puntland): 100–180 mm — 10% below normal.الشمال: 100–180 ملم — تحت المعدل.Kaskazini: 100–180 mm.
  • Kulayska: 0.5°C ka sare caadi — beeralayda waa inay isticmaalaan mulching iyo wararada subax/galab.Temperatures: 0.5°C above average — use mulching and morning/evening watering.الحرارة: 0.5°م فوق المعدل.Joto: 0.5°C juu ya kawaida.

Talooyinka Beeralayda Soomaaliyeed Recommendations for Somali farmers توصيات للمزارعين Mapendekezo kwa wakulima

  • Bilow beerista 5–10 Oktoobar — abaarka jiifsiga waxa la filayaa inuu yimaado bartamaha Oktoobar.Start planting 5–10 October — first rains expected mid-October.ابدأ الزراعة في أوائل أكتوبر.Anza kupanda mwanzo wa Oktoba.
  • Dooro dalagga xilli-gaaban (90–110 maalmood): masaggu, digirta, salaaqada — xilligu wuu gaaban yahay sannadkan.Choose short-cycle crops (90–110 days): sorghum, cowpeas, sesame — season is shorter this year.اختر محاصيل قصيرة الدورة.Chagua mazao ya muda mfupi.
  • Diyaari biyo-kayd (boqolaal ama berkado yar) — Diseembar dhammaadkiisa ayaa qallayl jiraa.Prepare water storage (cisterns or small reservoirs) — December dry spell expected.جهّز خزانات مياه.Andaa hifadhi ya maji.

Khataraha la sii diyaarsanayo Risks to plan for المخاطر Hatari

Kulayska sii kordhaya wuxuu kor u qaadaa khatarta cudurka Fall Armyworm oo galleyda iyo masaggu daawayn karta. Beeralayda waa inay si dhakhso ah iska indho-indheeyaan oo helaan daawooyinka biological-ka oo lagu helo suuqyada gobolka. Sidoo kale, ka soo dhaca Locust waa hoos u dhacay 70% — laakiin Beera-laanta waxay sii indhayn doontaa Oktoobar. Higher temperatures increase risk of Fall Armyworm on maize and sorghum. Farmers should scout weekly and access biological controls available in regional markets. Locust activity is down 70% but Ministry of Agriculture will continue monitoring through October. ارتفاع الحرارة يزيد خطر دودة الحشد الخريفية. الجراد انخفض 70٪. Joto kupanda huongeza hatari ya Fall Armyworm.
