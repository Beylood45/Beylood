Cover Crops — Dalagga DaboolahaCover Crops — Healing the Soil Between Seasonsمحاصيل الغطاءMazao ya Kufunika

2026-05-237 daqiiqo7 min read7 دقائقdakika 7

Marka aad gurato dalagga aasaasiga ah, dhulka oo bannaan maaha guul — ciidda way nabaad-guurta, way qalashaa, oo nafaqada way lumin. Cover crops (dalagga daboolaha) waxay daboolan dhulka, ku biiriyaan nitrogen-ka bilaashka ah, oo kor u qaadaan wax-soosaarka dalagga xiga 10–30%. Bare soil between crops erodes, dries out, and loses nutrients. Cover crops protect the soil, fix free nitrogen, and lift the next cash crop's yield 10–30%. التربة العارية تتعرّى وتفقد المغذيات. محاصيل الغطاء تحمي التربة وتثبت النيتروجين وترفع إنتاج المحصول التالي 10–30%. Udongo wazi hupoteza virutubisho. Cover crops huongeza mavuno 10–30%.

Faa'iidooyinka Saddexda AasaasiThree Core Benefitsالفوائد الثلاثFaida Kuu Tatu

  • 1) Nitrogen bilaash ah — digirta (legumes) waxay ku xidhi karaan 50–200 kg N/ha hawada — bedelka bacrinta urea.1) Free nitrogen — legumes fix 50–200 kg N/ha from the atmosphere — replacing urea purchases.1) نيتروجين مجاني — البقوليات تثبت 50–200 كغ نيتروجين/هكتار.1) Naitrojeni bure — mikunde huongeza kg 50–200/hektari.
  • 2) Ka hortagga nabaad-guurka — caleemaha iyo xididada cover crop-ka waxay ka difaacaan ciidda roob iyo dabaysha — yarayn 70–95% nabaad-guurka.2) Erosion control — canopy and roots shield soil from rain and wind — 70–95% less erosion.2) منع التعرية — تحمي 70–95% من التعرية.2) Kuzuia mmomonyoko — 70–95%.
  • 3) Suppress weeds — daboolaha dhirta dhaqaaqa cawska xun ee hooseeya wuxuu yarayn karaa 60–80% cawska beerta xigta.3) Weed suppression — dense canopy shades out weeds, cutting them 60–80% in the next crop.3) كبت الأعشاب — يقلل الأعشاب 60–80%.3) Kuzuia magugu — 60–80%.

Goorma loo Beero?When to Plantمتى نزرعLini Kupanda

Bilow gu'ga: isla markaad guratay dalagga deyrta, beero cover crop-ka kahor inta aanan jiilaalku gaadhin. Intercrop: kala dhex beer cover crop-ka iyo dalagga aasaasiga ah (tusaale: galley + cowpea). Daboolaha xilliga jiilaalka: waxa loo isticmaalaa noocyada abaaraha ka adkaysta (cowpea, lablab). After harvest: sow immediately after the cash crop is harvested, before dry season. Intercrop: grow cover crops between rows of the main crop (e.g., maize + cowpea). Dry-season cover: use drought-tolerant species (cowpea, lablab). بعد الحصاد: ازرع فوراً. Baada ya mavuno au kuotesha.

Sida loo Joojiyo (Termination)Terminationإنهاء الزراعةKumaliza

Joojin cover crop-ka kahor inta uusan iniin gelin si aysan u noqon caws aafo ah. Habab: Mowing oo daa caleemaha sida mulch ah. Roller-crimper oo cabsi geliya dhirta. Falaarta gaaban ee dhirta dhulka ku gasha. No-till transplanting — beero dalagga xiga toos ah daboolka cover crop-ka. Terminate before cover crop sets seed so it doesn't become a weed. Methods: mowing and leaving residue as mulch; roller-crimper; shallow tillage; no-till transplanting directly into the residue. أنهِها قبل البذور: قص أو حراثة سطحية أو زراعة مباشرة. Maliza kabla ya mbegu: kata au lima.
