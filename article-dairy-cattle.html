Lo'da Caanaha — Hagaha BuuxaDairy Cattle — The Complete Guideأبقار الحليب — الدليل الشاملNg'ombe wa Maziwa — Mwongozo Kamili

2026-05-2610 daqiiqo10 min read10 دقائقdakika 10

Lo'da caanaha waa dakhli joogto ah, oo ka shaqaysa cuntada qoyska iyo bacrinta beerta (digada). Lo'da Soomaaliyeed (Zebu) waxay siisaa 3–8 litir maalintii, halka lo'da xilliga (Friesian × Zebu) gaadhsiisaan 15–25 litir. Dairy cows generate steady cash, feed the household, and fertilise the farm. Local Zebu cows give 3–8 L/day; improved crosses (Friesian × Zebu) reach 15–25 L/day. أبقار الحليب دخل ثابت. الزيبو 3–8 لتر، الهجين 15–25 لتر. Ng'ombe wa maziwa hutoa kipato cha kawaida.

Hooyga iyo XeraddaHousingالمأوىBanda

Sas kasta oo lo'ad ah: 4–6 m² oo gudaha xerada, 8–10 m² oo open paddock. Saqaf ayaa ka difaaca qorraxda iyo roobka. Dhulka: simented ama dhul gargooyo + saxan. Drainage: dareenka oo ka durko jiifka. Wakhtiga jiilaalka, dabayl wanaagsan oo ka difaca kulkulka. Per cow: 4–6 m² of indoor space plus 8–10 m² open paddock. Roof for shade and rain. Floor: concrete or compacted earth, sloped for drainage. In hot months, ensure cross-ventilation to prevent heat stress. 4–6 م² داخلي + 8–10 م² خارجي للبقرة. 4–6 m² ndani na 8–10 m² nje.

Cuntada (Feed)FeedingالتغذيةLishe

Lo'da caano-bixisa maalintii waxay rabtaa: 40 kg caws qoyaan (ama 12–15 kg qalalan) + 2–4 kg ifrale (galley, digirta, sun cake) + 100 g macdano iyo cusbo. Litir kasta oo caanaha + 0.5 kg ifrale dheeraad ah. Biyo nadiif ah 50–80 litir maalintii. A milking cow needs daily: 40 kg wet forage (or 12–15 kg dry) + 2–4 kg concentrate (maize, dairy meal, soybean cake) + 100 g mineral lick. Add 0.5 kg concentrate per litre of milk. Clean water: 50–80 L/day. 40 كغ علف رطب + 2–4 كغ مركز + 100 غ معادن، 50–80 لتر ماء. kg 40 nyasi mvua + 2–4 nafaka + 100 g madini.

Caws-quudin caanka ah: Napier grass (1.5–2 m dheer, soo saari 200 t/ha sannadkii), Rhodes grass, masaggada quudinta, lablab, iyo Calliandra (geed digir ah). Top forages: Napier (200 t/ha/year), Rhodes grass, forage sorghum, lablab, Calliandra (fodder tree). الأعلاف — نابير، رودس، ذرة العلف. Nyasi bora — Napier, Rhodes.

Taranka iyo Daryeelka CaanahaBreeding and Calvingالتزاوج والولادةKuzaa

Galmada ku habboon: 15–18 bilood (sas), 350 kg miisaan. Uur: 9 bilood iyo 7 maalmood. Caano: 305 maalmood lactation + 60 maalmood "dry period" (qabowga sas-da). AI (Artificial Insemination) waa ugu fiican — wuxuu siiyaa irmaan ka fiican ee local bull-ka. First breeding: 15–18 months and at least 350 kg. Gestation: 9 months 7 days. 305-day lactation + 60-day dry period. Artificial insemination (AI) gives genetics far better than the local bull. أول إخصاب: 15–18 شهراً، 350 كغ. الحمل 9 أشهر و7 أيام. Mara ya kwanza: miezi 15–18, kg 350.

Lisha iyo Kaydinta CaanahaMilking and Storageالحلب والتخزينKukamua na Kuhifadhi

Lis laba jeer maalintii (5 AM iyo 5 PM) si caanaha loo cusboonaysiiyo. Nadiif: gacmaha, baaldiyada, iyo idin. Caanaha kaydi 4°C 4 saac gudahood (qaboojiye ama jar oo biyaha qaboow gelinaya). Caanaha aan la qaboojin ku jirta 2 saac gudahood waxay noqotaa biya — khasaaray. Milk twice daily (5 AM and 5 PM). Wash hands, buckets, and udder. Chill milk to 4°C within 4 hours (cooler or jar in cold water). Unchilled milk spoils in 2 hours. الحلب مرتين، تبريد لـ4°م خلال 4 ساعات. Kamua mara mbili, baridi hadi 4°C.

DhaqaalahaEconomicsالاقتصادUchumi

Lo'da Friesian × Sahiwal ee 15 L maalintii (305 maalmood) = 4,575 L sannadkii. Hadii litirku $0.50 = $2,287 sannadkii. Costs: cunto $1,000, dawooyin $150, hooyga $200 = $1,350. Faa'iidada: ~$900 lo'da kasta sannadkii — plus sas iyo digo. A Friesian × Sahiwal cow giving 15 L/day across 305 days = 4,575 L/year. At $0.50/L = $2,287 revenue. Costs: feed $1,000, vet $150, housing $200 = $1,350. Net: ~$900 per cow per year — plus a calf and free manure. دخل سنوي 2,287$، صافي 900$ + عجل. Mapato $900/mwaka kwa ng'ombe.
