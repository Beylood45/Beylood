Beerista BakeylahaRabbit Farmingتربية الأرانبUfugaji wa Sungura

2026-05-186 daqiiqo6 min6 دقائقdakika 6

Bakeylaha (rabbit) waxay siiyaa hilib protein sare leh (21%, dheef yar) iyada oo isticmaalaya dhul iyo cunto yar. Hooyo 1 ah sannadkii waxay siisaa 30–40 ilmo oo gaaraya 2–2.5 kg 12 toddobaad gudahood — dakhli dhakhso ah.Rabbits produce lean high-protein meat (21%) using little land or feed. One doe produces 30–40 kits/year, reaching 2–2.5 kg in 12 weeks — quick returns.الأرانب — لحم بروتين عالٍ.Sungura — protini juu.

NoocyadaBreedsالسلالاتAina

  • New Zealand White — caafimaad sare, miisaan dheelitiran (4 kg).New Zealand White — healthy, balanced 4 kg.نيوزيلندي أبيض.New Zealand.
  • California — koritaan dhakhso, hilib sare.California — fast growth, top meat.كاليفورنيا.California.
  • Flemish Giant — weyn (6–7 kg), tartiib ah.Flemish Giant — large (6–7 kg), slower.الفلمنكي العملاق.Flemish.

HooygaHousingالمسكنBanda

Hutch (cage) 1 m × 0.5 m × 0.5 m bakeyl kasta. Sagxadda: wire 14 mm si digada iyo kaadiga ay ka dhacaan. Hawo wanaagsan, hoos, ka fog dabaysha. Heerkulka ugu fiican 12–22°C — qorraxda kulul (>30°C) waxay disha bakeylaha.Hutch 1 m × 0.5 m × 0.5 m per rabbit. Wire floor (14 mm) for waste drop-through. Good airflow, shade, no draft. Optimal temperature 12–22°C — heat above 30°C kills rabbits.قفص لكل أرنب.Tundu kwa kila sungura.

CuntadaFeedالتغذيةLishe

Pellet 16–18% protein (100–150 g/maalin) + caws qoyaan iyo qalalan (alfalfa, Napier) bilaash. Khudaarta (qashinka beerta): basasha, caws, cabbage — yar. Biyo nadiif 200–400 ml/maalin.Pellet 16–18% protein (100–150 g/day) + ad-lib green forage (alfalfa, Napier). Vegetable scraps (limited). Clean water 200–400 ml/day.علف 16–18% بروتين.Pellet 16–18%.

TarankaBreedingالتزاوجKuzaa

Hooyo bilow taran: 5–6 bilood. Uur: 30–32 maalmood. Litter: 6–10 ilmo. Bedeli karo 7 jeer sannadkii. Aab 1 → 10 hooyooyin.Doe ready at 5–6 months. Gestation: 30–32 days. Litter: 6–10 kits. Up to 7 litters/year. One buck per 10 does.الحمل 30 يوماً.Mimba siku 30.

SuuqaMarketالسوقSoko

Hilibka: $5–$10/kg suuqa makhaayadaha. Bakeyl 2 kg = $10–$20. Doe 1 sannadkii: 30 ilmo × $15 = $450. Maalgashi yar (3 doe + buck + 4 hutches = $300) waxay ku siisaa $1,200+ sannadkii.Meat: $5–$10/kg in restaurants. 2 kg rabbit = $10–$20. One doe yields 30 kits × $15 = $450/year. Low capital ($300 setup) → $1,200+ income.هامش 1,200$ سنوياً.Faida $1,200/mwaka.
