Xajka 2026: 4.5 Milyan Xoolo oo Soomaaliyeed oo Sucuudiga La Dhoofin DoonoHajj 2026: 4.5 Million Somali Livestock to Be Exported to Saudi Arabiaالحج 2026: 4.5 مليون رأس صومالي إلى السعوديةHajj 2026: Mifugo 4.5 Milioni

2026-05-304 daqiiqo4 min4 دقائقdakika 4

Wasaaradda Beeraha Soomaaliya iyo wasiirka ganacsiga Sucuudiga waxay sii sheegeen in xilliga Xajka 2026 (Juun) la sii diyaarinayo dhoofinta 4.5 milyan oo xoolo (riyo, ido, lo'da, geela). Qiimaha hal ilmo ah waxa la filayaa $180–$300 — record sare ka mid ah 5-da sano ee la soo dhaafay. Somalia's Ministry of Agriculture and Saudi commerce officials announced preparations to export 4.5 million animals (goats, sheep, cattle, camels) for Hajj 2026 (June). Per-head prices expected at $180–$300 — a 5-year high. الصومال ستصدر 4.5 مليون رأس للحج 2026. Mifugo milioni 4.5 itasafirishwa.
