Vermicompost — Compost-ka DirxigaVermicomposting — Worm Compostالكمبوست الدوديMboji ya Minyoo

2026-05-186 daqiiqo6 min6 دقائقdakika 6

Vermicompost — compost-ka uu dirxigu sameeyo — waa bacrin sare oo leh nafaqo 5–10 jeer ka badan compost caadi ah. Wuxuu ka dhigaa dalagga adkaysi leh cudur, biyaha qabsada, oo soo saari karta dakhli ganacsi.Vermicompost — compost made by worms — is premium fertiliser with 5–10× more available nutrients than regular compost. Improves disease resistance, water-holding, and can be sold commercially.كمبوست الديدان أعلى مغذيات 5–10 أضعاف.Mboji ya minyoo bora 5–10×.

Noocyada DirxigaWorm Speciesأنواع الديدانAina za Minyoo

Eisenia fetida (Red Wriggler) waa noocyada ugu fiican. Waxay cunaan 50% miisaankooda maalintii oo waxay isku tarmaaan 1 → 1,000 sannad gudahood. Heerkulka ugu fiican: 18–25°C. pH: 6–8.Eisenia fetida (Red Wriggler) is the top species. They eat 50% of their body weight daily and multiply 1,000-fold in a year. Optimal temperature 18–25°C, pH 6–8.Eisenia fetida أفضل نوع.Eisenia fetida ni bora.

Dhismaha Bin-kaSetting Up the Binإنشاء الصندوقKutengeneza Sanduku

Sanduuq bedding (caws qalalan + waraaqo) 15 cm dheer, daboolaadkii hoose oo daloolada leh oo biyaha sii daysa. Geli 1 kg dirxiyo (2,000 ah) m² kasta. Quudin: qashin khudaarta, jiko, qalfoofka ukunta — laakiin HA cunin hilib, caano, ama saliid.Make a bin: 15 cm of bedding (dry grass + cardboard), bottom drains. Add 1 kg of worms (≈2,000) per m². Feed kitchen scraps, peels, eggshells — but NO meat, dairy, or oil.صندوق 15 سم.Sanduku 15 sm.

DaryeelkaCareالرعايةUtunzaji

  • Hubi qoyaanka 70–85% (sida hawada baniga).Keep moisture 70–85% (like a wrung sponge).الرطوبة 70–85%.Unyevu 70–85%.
  • Quudin 2–3 jeer asbuucii, ha cufin in dirxigu badbaadayo.Feed 2–3 times/week, don't overload.تغذية 2–3 مرات/أسبوع.Lisha mara 2–3.
  • Daboolaad bin-ka — dhal qaylaha qabowga iyo qorraxda kulul.Cover bin — block light, shield from cold/heat.غطِّ الصندوق.Funika sanduku.
  • Iska ilaali ammonia: hadii ay uri tahay, ku dar bedding cusub.Avoid ammonia: if it smells, add bedding.تجنب الأمونيا.Epuka amonia.

Soo SaaristaHarvestingالحصادKuvuna

3–6 bilood gudahood, bedding-ku wuxuu noqdaa compost bunni-madow ah. Habka: dhig nuska bin-ka, ku dar cunto cusub dhinaca kale — dirxiyada way u guurayaan. Saac 24 ka dib, hor u soo qaad compost-ka kaydsan.After 3–6 months, bedding becomes dark brown compost. Method: move bedding to one side and add fresh food on the other — worms migrate. After 24 hrs, scoop the finished compost.احصد بعد 3–6 أشهر.Vuna baada ya miezi 3–6.

Isticmaal iyo DakhligaUse and Salesالاستخدام والمبيعMatumizi

Isticmaal 5–10 t/ha beerta khudaarta ama 100 g godkii beerista. Suuqa Soomaaliya/Kenya: vermicompost iibinaayaa $200–$500/ton — markka labaad ka faa'iidamayaasha ugu fiican xoolaha yaryar.Apply 5–10 t/ha to vegetable plots or 100 g per planting hole. Market price in Somalia/Kenya: $200–$500/ton — one of the best returns for small enterprises.السعر 200–500 دولار/طن.Bei $200–$500/tani.
