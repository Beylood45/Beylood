Furrow Irrigation — Waraabin SaraarFurrow Irrigationالري بالأخاديدUmwagiliaji wa Mifereji

2026-05-227 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Waraabinta saraarka (furrow) waxay biyaha mariyaa kanaalo yaryar oo u dhexeeya safafka dalagga. Waa habka ugu caansan dunida — qaabkiisu yar yahay, qiimihiisuna hooseeyaa, balse haddii la fiirsan ee uu xidho 25–40% biyaha.Furrow irrigation flows water along small channels between crop rows. The world's most common method — low-cost and easy, but inefficient (25–40% losses) without careful design.الري بالأخاديد — يوزع الماء بين الصفوف؛ شائع لكنه يفقد 25–40% بلا تصميم جيد.Umwagiliaji wa mifereji — wa kawaida lakini hupoteza 25–40%.

Goorta Loo IsticmaaloWhen to Use Furrowsمتى تستخدمLini Kutumia

Furrows waxay ku habboon yihiin dalagga safaf ah (galley, basasha, yaanyada, qaraha), dhul sallaxan ama jiif yar (slope <3%), iyo meelaha biyaha laga keeno wabi ama ceel awood sare leh.Furrows suit row crops (maize, onions, tomato, watermelon), flat or gently sloped land (<3% slope), and sites with abundant river or well water.للمحاصيل الصفية والأراضي المسطحة.Kwa mazao ya safu.

Tilmaamaha NaqshaddaDesign Guidelinesمعايير التصميمVigezo vya Kupanga

  • Dhererka saraarka: 50–200 m (kor u dhererka ciidda ka mid ah).Furrow length: 50–200 m (longer in heavier clay soils).الطول — 50–200 م.Urefu — m 50–200.
  • Masaafada saraarka: waafaqsan masaafada dalagga (60–90 cm khudaarta, 75–100 cm galleyda).Spacing: match crop row spacing (60–90 cm vegetables, 75–100 cm maize).التباعد — حسب الصف.Nafasi — sawa na safu.
  • Slope: 0.2–0.5% ugu fiican; aad u hooseeya = biyaha taagan, aad u sareeya = nabaad-guur.Slope: 0.2–0.5% ideal — too flat ponds water, too steep erodes.الانحدار — 0.2–0.5%.Mteremko — 0.2–0.5%.
  • Inflow rate: 0.5–2 L/s saraar kasta — biyaha gaadhsiisaan dhamaadka 25–50% wakhtiga waraabinta.Inflow rate: 0.5–2 L/s per furrow — water should reach the end in 25–50% of total irrigation time.التدفق — 0.5–2 لتر/ثانية.Mtiririko.

Hagaajinta Wax-soosaarkaImproving Efficiencyتحسين الكفاءةKuboresha

Saraar kasta oo loo isticmaalo waraabinta, ka tag saraarka kale qalalan (alternate furrow irrigation) — kaydiya 30% biyaha iyada oo aan la dhimin wax-soosaarka. Daboolaad caws qalalan dhulka ka difaaca uumiga. Surge irrigation (biyaha geli dabakhsanaan ah) waxay yarayn kartaa khasaaraha 15–25%.Use alternate-furrow irrigation (water every other furrow) — saves 30% with no yield loss. Mulch dry grass between rows to cut evaporation. Surge irrigation (pulsing water on/off) reduces deep losses 15–25%.تناوب الأخاديد يوفر 30%.Mfumo wa kupishana huokoa 30%.

DaryeelkaMaintenanceالصيانةMatengenezo

  • Dib u sameey saraarka beerta kasta — falaarta yarayn kartaa qotada.Re-form furrows each season — repeated tillage reduces depth.أعد تشكيل الأخاديد كل موسم.Tengeneza upya kila msimu.
  • Iska saar cawska kanaalka biyaha si biyaha si fiican u socdaan.Clear weeds from main channels for steady flow.أزل الأعشاب من القنوات.Ondoa magugu mifereji.
  • Tijaabi hal saraar kahor waraabinta — qiyaas wakhtiga ay biyaha u qaataan gaadhsiinta dhamaadka.Test one furrow first — time how long water takes to reach the end.اختبر أخدوداً واحداً أولاً.Jaribu mfereji mmoja.
