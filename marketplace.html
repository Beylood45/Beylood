Suuqa BeerahaAgricultural Marketplaceالسوق الزراعيSoko la Kilimo

Mustaqbalka, beeraleydu waxay halkan ku soo bandhigi doonaan dalagyadooda, abuurka, qalabka iyo adeegyada beeraha — si iibsadayaasha iyo iibiyeyaashu si toos ah isugu xirmaan, dhexdhexaadin la'aan.In the future, farmers will list their crops, seed, equipment and farm services here — connecting buyers and sellers directly, without middlemen.مستقبلاً سيعرض المزارعون هنا محاصيلهم والبذور والمعدات والخدمات الزراعية — لربط المشترين والبائعين مباشرة دون وسطاء.Siku zijazo, wakulima wataorodhesha hapa mazao yao, mbegu, vifaa na huduma za kilimo — kuunganisha wanunuzi na wauzaji moja kwa moja, bila madalali.
