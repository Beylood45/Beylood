Beerista Shiniyaha — MalabkaBeekeeping — Honey Productionتربية النحلUfugaji wa Nyuki

2026-05-207 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Beerista shiniyaha waa ganacsi qiimo yar (qiimo bilow $50–$150 hive kasta) oo soo saara qiimo sare ($5–$15 malab kasta). Hive 1 ah sannadkii waxay soo saari kartaa 10–25 kg malab + bayswax. Plus: shiniyaha ayaa biyada beerta — wax-soosaarka khudaarta wuxuu kor u kacaa 20–30%.Beekeeping is low-cost ($50–$150/hive) with high-value output ($5–$15/kg honey). One hive yields 10–25 kg honey + beeswax per year. Bonus: bees pollinate crops — vegetable yields rise 20–30%.تربية النحل عائد عالٍ.Ufugaji wa nyuki — mapato makubwa.

Goobta iyo DaryeelkaSite and CareالموقعMahali

Goob nabad qabsato, ka fog xoolaha iyo dadka, agteeda biyo (200 m) iyo ubaxyo. Daboolaad hooska bilaha kululka. Buuxi biyaha kala duwan (cabidda). Tijaab hive-ka asbuuc kasta — eeg honey-comb, larvae, iyo cudurro (varroa mites, foul brood).Quiet site away from people and livestock, near water (200 m) and flowers. Shade in hot months. Provide shallow drinking dishes. Inspect weekly — check honeycomb, larvae, and disease (varroa mites, foul brood).موقع هادئ وقريب من الزهور.Mahali tulivu.

Sida loo Soo Saaro MalabkaHoney Harvestحصاد العسلKuvuna Asali

Soo saar marka 75% qaybta honey-comb-ka ay xidhantahay (capped). Wakhtiyada ugu fiican: deyrku (Oktoobar–Disembar). Isticmaal smoker si shiniyaha loo dejiyo. Ka soo saar kuwa furi yaqaan (uncapping). Ku ridi qalad si la kala saaro malab + bayswax. Kayd: jaag glass ah, qaboojin sare ka dhaadhic.Harvest when 75% of comb is capped. Best season: Deyr (Oct–Dec). Use a smoker to calm bees. Uncap and extract centrifugally. Store in glass jars in a cool place.احصد عند ختم 75%.Vuna asilimia 75.
