Waraabinta Greenhouse-kaGreenhouse Irrigationري البيوت المحميةUmwagiliaji wa Greenhouse

2026-05-217 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Greenhouse waxay biyo aad u yar isticmaashaa marka loo eego beerta furan — laakiin saxnaanta cadaadiska iyo wakhtiga waa muhiim. Habab automation ah waxay siiyaan wax-soosaar caafimaad qaba 365 maalmood sannadkii.Greenhouses use far less water than open fields — but precise pressure and timing are essential. Automated drip with fertigation supports year-round high-value production.البيوت المحمية تستخدم ماءً أقل لكن بدقة عالية.Greenhouses hutumia maji kidogo lakini kwa usahihi.

Habka Ugu Fiican: Drip + FertigationBest System: Drip + Fertigationالأفضل: تنقيط + تغذيةBora: Drip + Fertigation

Drip line geed kasta (1 emitter 30 cm), cadaadis 0.8–1.5 bar, emitters 2–4 L/saac. Venturi ama dosing pump waxay ku darayaan bacrinta biyaha sida tasaado. Saxnaanta nafaqada: 1.5–2.5 dS/m EC, 5.5–6.5 pH.One emitter per plant (every 30 cm), 0.8–1.5 bar, 2–4 L/hr emitters. A Venturi or dosing pump injects fertiliser into the water. Nutrient targets: 1.5–2.5 dS/m EC, pH 5.5–6.5.1 قاطر لكل نبتة بضغط 0.8–1.5 بار.Emitter moja kwa mmea.

  • Soil moisture sensor — sii daa waraabinta marka qoyaanku hoosaado heer (15–25%).Soil moisture sensor — triggers irrigation when soil moisture dips below threshold.حساس الرطوبة.Sensor ya unyevu.
  • Climate controller — la xidha heerkulka, qoyaanka, iyo waraabinta — wax-soosaar 30% sare.Climate controller — integrates temperature, humidity, and irrigation — yields 30% higher.تحكم بالمناخ.Kidhibiti hali ya hewa.
