Hortagga Cudurrada XoolahaLivestock Disease Preventionالوقاية من أمراض الماشيةKuzuia Magonjwa

2026-05-167 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Cudurrada xoolaha waxay khaarjisaa beerta 15–40% sannadkii. Hortagid waa 10× ka khafiif daawayn — biosecurity, tallaalka, iyo daryeelka caafimaadka waa muhiim.Diseases cost livestock farms 15–40% annually. Prevention is 10× cheaper than treatment — biosecurity, vaccination, and routine health care are the keys.الوقاية أرخص 10 أضعاف من العلاج.Kuzuia ni bora kuliko kutibu.

TallaalkaVaccinationالتطعيمChanjo

  • Lo'da: FMD, anthrax, blackleg, lumpy skin, brucellosis.Cattle: FMD, anthrax, blackleg, lumpy skin, brucellosis.الأبقار.Ng'ombe.
  • Riyaha/Idaha: PPR, CCPP, sheep/goat pox, enterotoxaemia.Sheep/Goats: PPR, CCPP, sheep/goat pox, enterotoxaemia.الأغنام والماعز.Mbuzi na Kondoo.
  • Digaag: Newcastle, Marek's, Gumboro, Fowl Pox.Poultry: Newcastle, Marek's, Gumboro, Fowl Pox.الدواجن.Kuku.
  • Geela: camel pox, anthrax, trypanosomiasis prevention.Camels: camel pox, anthrax, trypanosomiasis prophylaxis.الإبل.Ngamia.

BiosecurityBiosecurityالأمن الحيويBiosecurity

  • Quarantine xoolaha cusub 30 maalmood ka hor inta aanad ku darin.Quarantine new animals 30 days before mixing.حجر 30 يوماً.Karantini siku 30.
  • Nadiifi xeradda asbuuc kasta — disinfectant + lakab cusub oo subkaya.Clean barns weekly — disinfect and refresh bedding.تنظيف أسبوعي.Safisha kila wiki.
  • Daboolaad xerada xoolaha kale — cudurka virus-ka sida FMD waxay faafan karaan dabaysha 50 km.Fence out neighbouring herds — viral diseases like FMD spread by wind 50 km.اعزل القطعان.Tenga makundi.
  • Maaree dadka soo gala — boots + disinfecting mat.Control visitor access — boots and a foot bath.تحكم بالزوار.Dhibiti wageni.

Daryeelka Caadiga ahRoutine CareالرعايةUtunzaji

  • Deworming: lo' 3 bilood kasta, riyo/idaha bilood ah xilliga qoyaan, digaag intuu yarahay.Deworm: cattle every 3 months, sheep/goats every month in wet season, chicks while young.ديدنة دورية.Dawa ya minyoo.
  • Tick/lice control: bilood kasta dipping ama spray.Tick/lice control: monthly dip or spray.مكافحة شهرية.Kupe — kila mwezi.
  • Vet checkup: 6 bilood kasta.Vet checkup every 6 months.فحص بيطري.Daktari kila miezi 6.
