Riyaha iyo Idaha — Hagaha BuuxaGoats and Sheep — The Complete Guideالماعز والأغنام — الدليل الشاملMbuzi na Kondoo — Mwongozo

2026-05-249 daqiiqo9 min read9 دقائقdakika 9

Riyaha iyo idaha (Small Ruminants) waa hantida khaasiska ah ee bulshada Soomaaliyeed iyo Geeska Afrika. Waxay siiyaan hilib, caano, dhogor, iyo dakhli ku-takriifid (suuq sare oo Khaliijka ah). Mid riyo ah baan ku noqon karaa hal dameer mid kasta — yarayn maalgashi. Goats and sheep are the cornerstone wealth of Somali and Horn-of-Africa society. They provide meat, milk, skins, and a strong export market (Gulf states). One pregnant doe pays for two by next season — low capital, high turnover. الماعز والأغنام ركيزة الثروة الصومالية. لحم، حليب، جلود، وتصدير للخليج. Mbuzi na kondoo ni utajiri muhimu.

  • Riyaha Galla (Somali Galla) — cad oo waaweyn (50–70 kg lab; 40–55 kg dheddig); siisa 2–3 L caano maalintii — hilib + caano-bixin.Galla goat — white, large (50–70 kg male; 40–55 kg female); 2–3 L milk/day — dual meat + milk.غالا — لحم وحليب.Galla — nyama na maziwa.
  • Black Head Persian Sheep (Idaha madow madaxa) — adkaystaa kulayl iyo abaaro, hilib tayo sare leh, siisa 1.0–1.2 ilmo dhalashada kasta.Black Head Persian sheep — heat- and drought-tolerant, premium meat quality, 1.0–1.2 lambs per kidding.الرأس الأسود الفارسي — لحم ممتاز.Black Head Persian.
  • Somali Berbera Blackhead — qiimo sare oo suuqa Khaliijka, kulayl ka adkaystaa, siisa ilmo 4 jeer 3 sano gudahood (3 dhalashado).Somali Berbera Blackhead — premium Gulf export price, heat-hardy, 3 lambings in 2–3 years.بربرة سوداء الرأس — تصدير ممتاز للخليج.Berbera Blackhead.
  • Boer (sii dheer) — riyo hilib (South African); kaboor 35 kg 5 bilood — ku habboon dadka loo dhaqo aagga roobka.Boer (imported) — meat goat (South African); 35 kg at 5 months — fits wetter zones.Boer — للحم.Boer — nyama.

HooygaHousingالمسكنBanda

Sadex hab caan ah: Open paddock (dhul daaq oo dabar la go'an), Semi-intensive (xero lagu hayaa habeenkii), iyo Intensive (zero-grazing). Mitir-laba-jibaaran kasta: 1.5–2 m² riyo ama ido kasta. Sare/dheer: 1.5 m sare. Sagxadda: kor u taagan 30 cm (slatted) si kaadi iyo digo ay u dhacaan — yaraysa parasites-ka. Three systems: open paddock (fenced grazing), semi-intensive (housed at night), intensive zero-grazing. Per animal: 1.5–2 m². Height: 1.5 m. Use a raised slatted floor (30 cm off ground) so dung and urine drop through — cuts parasites dramatically. ثلاثة أنظمة: مفتوح، شبه مكثف، مكثف. 1.5–2 م²/رأس. Mifumo mitatu. 1.5–2 m² kwa mnyama.

CuntadaFeedالتغذيةLishe

Riyaha iyo idaha waxay cunaan 3–4% miisaankooda jirka oo qalalan maalintii. Riyo 30 kg ah = 1 kg caws qalalan + 200 g ifrale (galleyda, masaggada, sun cake) + biyo 4–6 L + cusbo macdaneed. Riyaha siisa caano: 1 kg ifrale dheeraad ah kalalaad 2 L caano oo soo saari karta. Goats and sheep eat 3–4% of body weight as dry matter daily. A 30 kg goat: 1 kg dry forage + 200 g concentrate (maize, sorghum, sun cake) + 4–6 L water + mineral salt. A milking doe needs an extra 0.5 kg concentrate per litre of milk produced. 3–4% من الوزن مادة جافة يومياً. 3–4% ya uzito.

Riyaha waxay door bidaan caleemaha geedaha (browse) — Acacia, Leucaena, Calliandra. Idaha waxay door bidaan caws hooseeya (graze) — Rhodes, Napier. Goats prefer browse (tree leaves) — Acacia, Leucaena, Calliandra. Sheep prefer ground grass — Rhodes, Napier. الماعز ترعى الأشجار، الأغنام ترعى الأعشاب. Mbuzi hupendelea miti, kondoo nyasi.

TarankaBreedingالتزاوجKuzaa

Dhalashada ugu horeysa: 12–15 bilood. Uur: 145–155 maalmood (5 bilood). Habayno xilliyada inta u dhaxaysa (kidding interval): 8 bilood. Riyo 1 si caafimaad qabto: 5–6 ilmo (kids) 3 sano gudahood. Hubi inaad raqdid hal raqdo 25–30 dheddig. First mating: 12–15 months. Gestation: 145–155 days (5 months). Target kidding interval: 8 months. A healthy doe gives 5–6 kids in 3 years. Keep 1 buck per 25–30 does. أول تزاوج 12–15 شهراً. الحمل 145–155 يوماً. Mara ya kwanza miezi 12–15.

Daryeelka CaafimaadkaHealth CareالصحةAfya

  • Deworming: 3 bilood kasta — Haemonchus contortus (dirxiga dhiig liqa) ayaa khasaariya beero badan.Deworming: every 3 months — Haemonchus contortus (barber pole worm) kills many small ruminants.الديدان — كل 3 أشهر.Dawa ya minyoo — kila miezi 3.
  • Tallaalka: PPR (peste des petits ruminants — 'rinderpest yarta') sannadkii, CCPP, anthrax, enterotoxaemia.Vaccinations: PPR yearly, CCPP, anthrax, enterotoxaemia.التطعيمات — PPR، CCPP.Chanjo.
  • FAMACHA test — fiiri midabka isha si aad u ogaato cudur-dhiigta (anemia) cudurka dirxiga.FAMACHA test — check eye colour for worm-induced anaemia.FAMACHA — لون العين.FAMACHA.
  • Foot care — jar cufnaan kasta 2–3 bilood, ka hortag foot rot.Hoof care — trim every 2–3 months to prevent foot rot.العناية بالحوافر.Kucha.

Suuqa iyo DhaqaalahaMarket and Economicsالسوق والاقتصادSoko na Uchumi

Suuqyada gudaha (Hargeisa, Mogadishu, Kismaayo): $80–$150 dameer hilib ah. Suuqyada Khaliijka (Saudi, Yemen, Egypt) — siiba xilliga Xajka: $150–$300 dameer kasta. Beero leh 50 riyo waxay soo saari karan 50–80 dameer iibinta sannadkii, sannad-faa'iidada $4,000–$8,000 (oo aan kala saarin kharashka). Local markets (Hargeisa, Mogadishu, Kismaayo): $80–$150/head. Gulf export markets (Saudi, Yemen, Egypt) — especially Hajj season: $150–$300/head. A 50-doe flock produces 50–80 saleable animals/year — gross income $4,000–$8,000. الأسواق المحلية 80–150$، التصدير 150–300$. Soko la ndani $80–$150, nje $150–$300.
