Compost — Bacrinta Dabiici AhComposting — Organic Fertiliserالكمبوست — السماد العضويMboji — Mbolea Asili

2026-05-248 daqiiqo8 min read8 دقائقdakika 8

Compost-ku waa "dahabka madow" — bacrin dabiici ah oo lagu sameeyo qashinka beerta, jikada, iyo digada xoolaha. Wuxuu kor u qaada bacrinta carrada, yareeyaa qiimaha bacrinta kiimikada 50%, oo soo celiyaa nolosha ciidda (bacteria, fungi, worms). Compost is "black gold" — natural fertiliser made from crop waste, kitchen scraps, and manure. It builds soil fertility, cuts chemical-fertiliser costs by 50%, and restores soil biology (bacteria, fungi, worms). الكمبوست "الذهب الأسود" — سماد طبيعي من بقايا المحاصيل والمطبخ والسماد. يخصّب التربة ويوفر 50% من تكلفة الأسمدة. Mboji ni "dhahabu nyeusi" — mbolea asili ya taka za shamba.

C:N Ratio — Sirta GuushaThe C:N Ratio — Key to Successنسبة الكربون للنيتروجينUwiano wa C:N

Compost wuxuu u baahan yahay isku-dheelitir saxan ah oo u dhexeeya kaarboon (C, walxaha bunni qalalan) iyo nitrogen (N, walxaha cagaarka qoyan). Ratio ku habboon waa 30:1 (C:N). Aad u badan N: ur xun oo ammonia ah. Aad u badan C: si tartiib ah ayuu u kacaa. Compost needs balanced carbon (C, brown dry materials) and nitrogen (N, green wet materials). Target a 30:1 C:N ratio. Too much N: smells of ammonia. Too much C: decomposes very slowly. الكمبوست يحتاج توازن الكربون (مواد جافة) والنيتروجين (مواد خضراء). 30:1 مثالية. Mboji inahitaji uwiano wa C:N 30:1.

  • Bunni (Brown / C-high) — caws qalalan, caleemo qalalan, korog (sawdust), waraaqo, dad (300–500 C:N).Browns (high C) — dry grass, dry leaves, sawdust, cardboard, straw (300–500 C:N).البني — قش وأوراق جافة ونشارة (300–500 C:N).Kahawia — nyasi kavu, machujo (300–500).
  • Cagaar (Green / N-high) — qashinka khudaarta, caws cusub, digo, qashinka jikada, digirta (15–20 C:N).Greens (high N) — vegetable scraps, fresh grass, manure, kitchen waste, legume residues (15–20 C:N).الأخضر — قشور الخضار والسماد والقصاصات (15–20).Kijani — taka za mboga, samadi (15–20).

Habka Compost Kulul (Hot Composting)Hot Composting Methodالتخمير الحارNjia ya Mboji ya Joto

Pile ahaan 1.5 × 1.5 × 1.5 m. Lakab kasta: 10 cm bunni, 5 cm cagaar, 1 cm digada xoolaha (caawisa bacteria-da), iyo qoyaan yar. Ku celi ilaa pile-ku gaadho 1.5 mitir. Daboolaad: dhar madow ama caws qalalan. Heerkulkii pile-ku waa inuu gaadho 55–65°C 3 maalmood gudahood — tani waxay disho miraha cawska iyo cudurrada. Build a 1.5 × 1.5 × 1.5 m pile. Each layer: 10 cm browns, 5 cm greens, 1 cm manure (microbial starter), light watering. Repeat until 1.5 m tall. Cover with dark cloth or dry grass. The pile should hit 55–65°C within 3 days — kills weed seeds and pathogens. كومة 1.5×1.5×1.5 م. طبقات بنية وخضراء وسماد. 55–65°م تقتل البذور. Rundi 1.5×1.5×1.5 m. 55–65°C huua mbegu.

Rogrogashada: rog pile-ka 7-da maalmood kasta si oxygen-ka ugu galo. Diyaar 6–10 toddobaad gudahood marka uu pile-ku noqdo bunni madow, oo aan urin caaqil ahaan. Turning: turn the pile every 7 days for aeration. Ready in 6–10 weeks when material is dark brown, crumbly, and smells earthy. قلّب كل 7 أيام. جاهز خلال 6–10 أسابيع. Geuza kila wiki. Tayari wiki 6–10.

Vermicompost (Compost-ka Dirxiga)Vermicomposting (Worm Compost)الكمبوست الدوديMboji ya Minyoo

Isticmaal dirxiga noocyada Eisenia fetida (red wrigglers). Sanduuq dheer 30 cm: lakab caws ah, 1 kg dirxiyo, qashin jiko jiko. Heerkulka: 18–28°C. Vermicompost wuxuu leeyahay nafaqo 5–10 jeer ka badan compost caadi ah — bacrin sare oo loo isticmaalo khudaarta khasaaba iyo seedling-ka. Use Eisenia fetida (red wriggler) worms. A 30 cm deep box: layer of bedding grass, 1 kg worms, kitchen scraps. Keep at 18–28°C. Vermicompost contains 5–10× more available nutrients than regular compost — premium for high-value vegetables and seedlings. دود Eisenia fetida. صندوق 30 سم. مغذيات أعلى 5–10 أضعاف. Minyoo Eisenia fetida. Virutubisho 5–10× zaidi.

  • Ma kulayl → N yar ama qoyaan liita; ku dar caws cusub iyo biyo.Not heating up → too little N or moisture; add fresh greens and water.لا يسخن → أضف أخضر وماء.Haupashi → ongeza kijani na maji.

Isticmaalka Compost-kaUsing Compostاستخدام الكمبوستKutumia Mboji

Beerta ka hor inta aanad beerin: 10–20 t/ha (1–2 kg/m²) oo dheh ciidda 15 cm hoose. Khudaarta sasanaqa: 100 g godkii. Geedaha: 2–5 kg geed kasta sannadkii. Mulching: lakab 5 cm ah oo dhirta agteeda. Compost-ka wuxuu kor u qaadaa awoodda ciidda inay biyaha qabato 25% oo daciifsadaa cudurrada xididada. Pre-planting: 10–20 t/ha (1–2 kg/m²) mixed into top 15 cm. Veg seedlings: 100 g per planting hole. Trees: 2–5 kg per tree/year. Mulching: 5 cm layer around plants. Compost increases soil water-holding capacity 25% and suppresses root diseases. قبل الزراعة: 10–20 طن/هكتار. الأشجار: 2–5 كغ سنوياً. Kabla ya kupanda: tani 10–20/hektari.
