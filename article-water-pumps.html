Mootada biyaha waa qalbiga nidaamka waraabinta. Xulashada sax: dheellitir u dhexeeya qiimaha, awoodda, iyo ilaha tamarta (gaaska, korontada, qorraxda).The pump is the heart of any irrigation system. The right choice balances cost, capacity, and energy source (diesel, electricity, solar).المضخة قلب نظام الري.Pampu ni moyo wa mfumo.

Xisaabinta AwooddaSizing the Pumpحساب القدرةKuhesabu Uwezo

Awooda baahi: HP = (Q × H) / 76 / efficiency, halka Q = m³/s, H = mitir total head (taagga + cadaadiska). Tusaale: 5 L/s = 0.005 m³/s, head 30 m, efficiency 60% → 3.3 HP. Ku dar 25% safety margin.Power need: HP = (Q × H) / 76 / efficiency, where Q in m³/s and H is total head (lift + pressure). Example: 5 L/s × 30 m head ÷ 60% efficiency → 3.3 HP.
