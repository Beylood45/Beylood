Windbreaks — Ka Difaaca DabayshaWindbreaks for Farmsكاسرات الرياحVipenyo vya Upepo

2026-05-165 daqiiqo5 min5 دقائقdakika 5

Dabaysha kulul iyo qalalan ee Soomaaliya (Saaba, Haggaayo) waxay khaarbisaa caleemaha dalagga, qaadaa qoyaanka, oo nabaad-guur badan keenta. Windbreaks (geedaha xudduuda) waxay yarayn kartaa khasaaraha 50–80% oo waxay kor u qaadaan wax-soosaarka 20–30%.Hot dry winds (Saaba, Haggaayo) in Somalia tear leaves, strip moisture, and erode soil. Windbreaks (border trees) cut losses 50–80% and lift yields 20–30%.كاسرات الرياح تقلل الخسائر 50–80%.Vipenyo hupunguza hasara 50–80%.

NaqshadeyntaDesignالتصميمMuundo

Saf 2–3 ah oo geedaha dheer. Geed 1 ah wuxuu siiyaa difaac 10× sare. Geed 5 m = 50 m difaac downwind. Beero saf hooseeya (1–2 m), dhexdhexaad (3–5 m), iyo sare (6–10 m). Kala fog 2 m geedaha safka kasta.2–3 rows of tall trees. One tree shelters up to 10× its height — a 5 m tree protects 50 m. Mix heights: low (1–2 m), medium (3–5 m), tall (6–10 m). Trees 2 m apart per row.صفوف من 2–3.Safu 2–3.

Noocyada GeedahaTree SpeciesالأشجارAina za Miti

  • Casuarina equisetifolia — koritaan dhakhso ah, qoyaan-adkaysi leh, qaboojiyo.Casuarina — fast, salt- and drought-tolerant.Casuarina.Casuarina.
  • Eucalyptus — koritaan aad u dhakhso ah, qiimo qoryo.Eucalyptus — very fast, timber value.Eucalyptus.Eucalyptus.
  • Acacia tortilis — geedaha deegaanka, qaboojiyo iyo nabaad-guur ka difaaca.Acacia tortilis — native, drought-hardy and erosion control.Acacia.Acacia.
  • Leucaena leucocephala — koritaan dhakhso, quudin xoolaha.Leucaena — fast, fodder bonus.Leucaena.Leucaena.

DaryeelkaCareالرعايةUtunzaji

Waraabi 6–12 bilood ee ugu horeysa. Daboolaad guard (caws ama qodob) si xoolaha aysan u cunin. Jar caleemaha 3 sano kasta si geedaha aad ugu kor u kaco. Ka war hayso ininnka geedaha — qaarkood (Eucalyptus) waxay xididada kuwooda fog u baahdaan biyaha.Water for first 6–12 months. Protect young trees from livestock with brush. Prune every 3 years to encourage upward growth. Note root competition — Eucalyptus roots reach far for water.اروِ السنة الأولى.Mwagilia mwaka wa kwanza.
