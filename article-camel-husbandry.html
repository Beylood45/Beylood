Geela — Daryeelka iyo Wax-soosaarkaCamel HusbandryالإبلNgamia

2026-05-217 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Geelu waa hodanka Soomaaliyeed — Soomaaliya leh 7 milyan geel ah, koonteenka ugu badan adduunka. Caanaha geela waxay siiyaan nafaqo, dakhli ($1.5–$3/litir), iyo hilib qiimo leh. Si fiican u qaab-dhismeed ah, geel kasta sannadkii waxay siisaa $400–$1,200.Camels are Somali wealth — Somalia has 7 million camels, the largest population in the world. Camel milk provides nutrition, income ($1.5–$3/L), and quality meat. Properly managed, each camel earns $400–$1,200/year.الصومال أكبر مربي للإبل عالمياً.Somalia ina ngamia wengi zaidi.

Cuntada iyo BiyahaFeed and Waterالتغذية والماءLishe na Maji

Geelu wuu cuni karaa caws qudh-qudh leh, geedo (Acacia, Salvadora), iyo dhirta leh cusbo. Cunto: 8–12 kg dry matter/maalin. Biyo: geelu wuxuu cabbi karaa 100 litir 10 daqiiqo gudahood, oo wuxuu joogi karaa abaaro 10 maalmood biyo la'aan. Sii biyo 3–4 maalmood kasta.Camels graze tough grasses, browse trees (Acacia, Salvadora), and salt-bushes. Daily intake: 8–12 kg dry matter. Water: they can drink 100 L in 10 min and go 10 days without — water them every 3–4 days.8–12 كغ علف يومياً.kg 8–12 kwa siku.

Caanaha GeelaCamel Milkحليب الإبلMaziwa ya Ngamia

Geel hooyo ah: 5–15 litir maalintii (305 maalmood). Caano caafimaad sare leh: protein 3%, vit C 5× lo'da, insulin gargaaraha sonkorta. Suuqa gudaha: $1.5/L; Khaliij: $5–$10/L (export). Hubi nadaafadda — caano lo' Soomaaliya iibinaayaa 100,000+ litir Hargeysa keliya maalintii.A milking she-camel produces 5–15 L/day for 305 days. High-nutrition: 3% protein, 5× the vitamin C of cow milk, contains insulin (helps diabetics). Local: $1.5/L; Gulf export: $5–$10/L. Hargeisa alone moves 100,000+ L/day.5–15 لتر يومياً.Lita 5–15 kwa siku.
