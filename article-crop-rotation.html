Wareejinta DalaggaCrop Rotationالدورة الزراعيةMzunguko wa Mazao

2026-05-207 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Beerista hal dalag goob isku mid ah sannado badan waxay khaarjisaa nafaqada gaarka ah oo ay keentaa cudurro iyo cayayaan. Wareejinta dalagga waxay yarayn karaa bacrinta kiimikada 30–40% oo cudurrada xididada 50%.Planting the same crop in the same spot year after year strips specific nutrients and breeds pests/diseases. Rotation cuts chemical fertiliser need 30–40% and soil-borne diseases 50%.نفس المحصول يستنزف المغذيات.Zao moja huishia virutubisho.

Wareeg 4-Sano Tusaale4-Year Sample Rotationدورة 4 سنواتMzunguko wa Miaka 4

Sannad 1: Legumes (cowpea — ku biiriyo N). Sannad 2: Cereals (galley — isticmaala N). Sannad 3: Brassicas (kaabash — joojiyo cudurrada). Sannad 4: Solanaceae (yaanyada — leh nafaqo cusub). Ka dib, dib u bilow.Year 1: legumes (cowpea — adds N). Year 2: cereals (maize — uses N). Year 3: brassicas (cabbage — breaks pest cycles). Year 4: solanaceae (tomato — fresh nutrients). Then repeat.1: بقوليات، 2: حبوب، 3: صليبية، 4: باذنجانية.1: Mikunde, 2: Nafaka, 3: Brassica, 4: Solanaceae.

  • Ku raac dalagga deep-rooted (yaanyada) shallow-rooted ka (basasha).Follow deep-rooted (tomato) with shallow-rooted (onion).تناوب الجذور.Mizizi tofauti.

  • Ku celi cover crops xilliyada dhexdooda.Use cover crops between cash crops.محاصيل الغطاء بين الفصول.Mazao ya kufunika.
