FAO iyo SWALIM (UN) ayaa Maajo 2026 lagu sii sheegay in xilliga Gu' (Abriil–Juun) 2026 uu noqon doono mid roob badan oo kor u dhaafayaa celceliska — 120–140% oo celceliska 30 sano. Beeralayda gobolada Hiiraan, Bay, iyo Bakool waxay heli doonaan fursad weyn — laakiin ay khataro ku xidhan tahay. FAO and SWALIM (UN) announced in May 2026 that the Gu' season (April–June) 2026 will deliver above-average rainfall — 120–140% of the 30-year mean. Farmers in Hiiraan, Bay, and Bakool will benefit — but with real risks. FAO و SWALIM يتوقعون أمطاراً فوق المتوسط لـ Gu 2026. FAO na SWALIM wanatabiri mvua nyingi.
