## 0) MARKA UGU HORREYSA — Deploy & HTTPS ✅
Dadku ma arki karaan wax aan online ahayn.
1. Fayl kasta oo cusub geli Hostinger (`public_html`) — File Manager ama FTP.
2. Hubi `https://beylood.com` inuu si sax ah u shaqeeyo (HTTPS akhri, oon qalad lahayn).
3. **PDF-ka GAP:** geli galka, magaca ha noqdo `hab-beerashada-wanaagsan-gap.pdf` (badhannada download-ka).

## 1) GOOGLE SEARCH CONSOLE — halka traffic-ka ugu badan ka yimaado 🌍
Kani waa **tallaabada ugu muhiimsan.** 80%+ booqdayaasha waxay ka yimaadaan raadinta Google.

1. Aad: **https://search.google.com/search-console**
2. Ku gal `mohamedshafiemohamed@gmail.com`.
3. Ku dar hantida (Property) → dooro **"URL prefix"** → geli `https://beylood.com`.
4. Xaqiiji lahaanshaha (Verify): habka ugu fudud waa **HTML tag** — Google wuxuu ku siin doonaa `<meta name="google-site-verification" content="XXXX">`. Ii soo sheeg code-kaas, waxaan ku dari doonaa `index.html`. (Ama Hostinger DNS TXT.)
5. Marka la xaqiijiyo → **Sitemaps** → geli `sitemap.xml` → Submit.
6. Isticmaal **"URL Inspection"** si aad bogagga muhiimka ah u codsato indexin degdeg ah.

## 2) BING WEBMASTER TOOLS 🔎
1. Aad: **https://www.bing.com/webmasters**
2. Waxaad si toos ah uga soo dejin kartaa Google Search Console (import).
3. Gudbi `sitemap.xml`. (Bing = Yahoo/DuckDuckGo sidoo kale.)

## 3) GOOGLE ANALYTICS (GA4) 📊
Website-kaagu wuxuu leeyahay analytics. Xaqiiji inaad **GA4 ID** sax ah ku dhex haysid `analytics-config.js`, si aad u aragto meesha booqdayaashu ka yimaadaan iyo waxay akhriyaan.

---

## 4) SIDA LAGA SARE MARO GOOGLE (SEO) — waa diyaar, sii xoojiso
Waxa website-kaagu HADDA leeyahay: sitemap, robots.txt, meta unfua, Open Graph, JSON-LD (Article + Organization + **FAQPage cusub → rich results**), canonical, mobile/PWA, xawaare fiican.

Waxaad ku kordhin kartaa:
- **Backlinks (ugu muhiimsan):** ka codso websto/blogs beeraha & jaaliyadda Soomaaliyeed inay ku xidhaan (link). Wadaag social media (Facebook, X, WhatsApp groups beeraha). Backlink tayo sare = kalsooni Google.
- **Content joogto ah:** maqaal cusub 1–2 toddobaadkii. Google wuxuu jecel yahay sites nool.
- **Ereyada la raadiyo:** ku qor cinwaanno dadku raadiyaan ("goorma galley la beeraa", "cudurada digir", "how to grow sorghum in dry land").
- **Local SEO:** samee **Google Business Profile** haddii ganacsi jiro.

## 5) SIDA DADKA DUNIDA LOO SOO JIITO 🌐
- **Luqadaha:** hadda Ingiriis/Carabi/Swahili way jiraan — kordhiya gaadhista Bariga Afrika, Carabta, iyo caalamka. (Marka dambe: URL luqadeed gaar ah oo hreflang leh → ka sarreyn caalami weyn.)
- **Social + Video:** samee accounts (Facebook/Instagram/TikTok/YouTube) → geli talooyin gaagaaban → ku xidh website. Video beeraha si weyn ayey u faafaan.
- **Warside Email (Newsletter):** ururi email booqdayaasha → dib ugu soo celi.
- **WhatsApp/Telegram:** koox beeraleyda Soomaaliyeed → wadaag maqaallo.
- **Wadaag PWA-ga:** dadku waxay ku rakibi karaan telefoonka sida app — taasi keentaa dib-u-soo-noqosho.
