Beerista Kalluunka — AquacultureFish Farming — Aquacultureتربية الأسماكUfugaji wa Samaki

2026-05-198 daqiiqo8 min8 دقائقdakika 8

Beerista kalluunka (aquaculture) waxay biyo yar isticmaashaa, dhul yar, oo siiya wax-soosaar protein sare leh. Tilapia waxay gaadhaa 500–800 g 4–6 bilood gudahood; catfish 1 kg 6 bilood. Hektarkii berkad-ku waxay siiyaa 4–10 ton sannadkii.Fish farming uses little water and land but produces high-protein output. Tilapia reaches 500–800 g in 4–6 months; catfish 1 kg in 6 months. A 1 ha pond yields 4–10 tons/year.تربية الأسماك بمساحة قليلة.Ufugaji eneo dogo.

NoocyadaSpeciesالأنواعAina

  • Nile Tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus) — ugu fiican beero yaryar; cuntada planktons, cunaa pellet feed.Nile Tilapia — best for smallholders; eats plankton and pellet feed.البلطي النيلي.Tilapia.
  • African Catfish (Clarias gariepinus) — gariira kalluunka tilapia, ay rabaan biyo qoyaan ah.African Catfish — tougher than tilapia, tolerates low oxygen.السلور.Kambare.
  • Common Carp — qaboowga ku haboon, koritaan tartiib ah.Common Carp — cool-water, slower growth.الشبوط.Carp.

Noocyada BerkaddaPond TypesالأحواضMabwawa

Earthen pond: 200–1,000 m², 1.5–2 m hoose, qiimo $0.5/m². Concrete pond: $5–$15/m², gudaha dabar la go'an. Plastic tank: $300–$800, joog 100 kalluun.Earthen pond: 200–1,000 m², 1.5–2 m deep, $0.5/m². Concrete: $5–$15/m², easier control. Plastic tank: $300–$800, holds 100 fish.أحواض ترابية أو خرسانية.Mabwawa ya udongo au saruji.

Cuntada iyo KoritaankaFeeding and GrowthالتغذيةLishe

Pellet feed: 25–35% protein, quudin 3–4 jeer maalintii (3–5% miisaanka kalluunka). Tilapia: 1.5 kg cunto = 1 kg kalluun (FCR). Hubi heerkulka 25–32°C (tilapia), oxygen 5+ mg/L, pH 6.5–8.5.Pellet feed: 25–35% protein, feed 3–4×/day (3–5% body weight). Tilapia FCR: 1.5 kg feed → 1 kg fish. Keep water 25–32°C, oxygen 5+ mg/L, pH 6.5–8.5.25–35% بروتين.Protini 25–35%.

Daryeelka BiyahaWater ManagementالمياهMaji

  • Beddel 10% biyaha asbuuc kasta.Replace 10% of water weekly.استبدل 10% أسبوعياً.Badilisha 10% kila wiki.
  • Aerate marka oxygen-ku hoosaado.Aerate when oxygen drops.تهوية.Hewa.
  • Iska ilaali bacrinta beerta agteeda — sumo kalluunka.Avoid runoff from fertilised fields — kills fish.تجنب جريان الأسمدة.Epuka mbolea.
