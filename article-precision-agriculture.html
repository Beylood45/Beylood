Precision Agriculture — Beerista Saxda ahPrecision Agriculture — The Complete Guideالزراعة الدقيقة — الدليل الشاملPrecision Agriculture

2026-05-3010 daqiiqo10 min10 دقائقdakika 10

Beerista saxda ah (precision agriculture) waxay isticmaashaa GPS, sensors, drones, iyo AI si dhirta loo siiyo waxa ay si saxda ah u baahan tahay — biyo, bacrin, daawo, oo aan loo isticmaalin si gaadhi-gaadhi ah. Beero Maraykan, Yurub, Israel iyo Kenya waxay sannadkii kor u qaadeen wax-soosaarka 15–30% iyada oo qarashka hoos u dhacay 20%. Precision agriculture uses GPS, sensors, drones, and AI to give crops exactly what they need — water, fertiliser, pesticide — instead of broadcast application. Farms in the US, Europe, Israel, and Kenya have lifted yields 15–30% while cutting input costs 20%. الزراعة الدقيقة تستخدم GPS وأجهزة الاستشعار والطائرات بدون طيار والذكاء الاصطناعي لتقديم احتياجات المحاصيل بدقة. Precision agriculture hutumia GPS, sensors, drones, na AI kupandisha mavuno 15–30%.

Variable Rate Technology (VRT)Variable Rate Technology (VRT)تقنية المعدل المتغيرVRT

Tractors-ka casriga ah ee VRT leh waxay si toos ah ugu beddelaan qaddarka bacrinta ama biyaha mar kasta oo ay galaan aag cusub. Tusaale: aag wuxuu leeyahay nitrogen sare — tractor ayaa lagu siinayaa kaliya 50 kg/ha urea; aag liita 150 kg/ha. Wax-soosaarka galleyda way kacaa, oo bacrinta lumeysa hoos u dhacaa 30%. Modern VRT tractors auto-adjust fertiliser or water rates as they cross zone boundaries. Example: a high-nitrogen zone receives only 50 kg/ha urea; a depleted zone gets 150 kg/ha. Maize yields rise and wasted fertiliser drops 30%. الجرارات تعدل المعدل تلقائياً. Trekta hubadilisha kiwango.
