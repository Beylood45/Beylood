Sprinkler Irrigation — Hagaha BuuxaSprinkler Irrigation — The Complete Guideالري بالرش — الدليل الشاملUmwagiliaji wa Sprinkler — Mwongozo Kamili

2026-05-228 daqiiqo8 min8 دقائقdakika 8

Sprinkler irrigation waxay biyaha siisaa dalagga sida roob saamayn ah. Si fiican u shaqayn dhul ballaaran (0.5–10 hektar) iyo dalagga cereal-ka (galley, qamadi, bariis upland). Waxay siisaa dheelitir biyo iyo nadiifin caleemaha; balse waxay isticmaashaa biyo ka badan drip irrigation 20–40%.Sprinkler irrigation delivers water like rainfall. Best for medium-to-large fields (0.5–10 ha) and cereals (maize, wheat, upland rice). It distributes water evenly and rinses leaves — but uses 20–40% more water than drip.الري بالرش يوصل الماء كالمطر. مناسب لحقول 0.5–10 هكتار وللحبوب — يوزع الماء بالتساوي لكنه يستهلك ماءً أكثر 20–40% من التنقيط.Sprinkler hupeleka maji kama mvua. Bora kwa mashamba 0.5–10 hektari na nafaka — lakini hutumia maji 20–40% zaidi ya drip.

Noocyada Sprinkler-kaSprinkler Typesأنواع الرشاشاتAina za Sprinkler

  • Impact sprinkler — wareeg 360° ama qayb, baahisaa biya 10–30 m, ugu fiican beero waaweyn.Impact sprinklers — rotate 360° or part-circle, throw 10–30 m, ideal for large fields.Impact — تدور 360° وتغطي 10–30 م.Impact — huzunguka 360°, mita 10–30.
  • Micro-sprinkler — baahisaa biya 2–8 m, ugu fiican geedaha miraha iyo nursery-ga.Micro-sprinklers — 2–8 m throw, perfect for orchards and nurseries.Micro — 2–8 م، للأشجار والمشاتل.Micro — m 2–8, kwa miti.
  • Centre-pivot — qalab waaweyn oo wareega goob; 30–60 hektar — beero ganacsiyada.Centre-pivot — large rotating system; 30–60 ha — for commercial farms.محوري مركزي — 30–60 هكتار للمزارع التجارية.Centre-pivot — kibiashara.
  • Hose-end / portable — qaab khafiif ah oo wakhti yar lagu rakibo, dadka beero yaryar leh.Hose-end portable — lightweight, quick setup, smallholder favourite.محمول — للمزارع الصغيرة.Hose-end — wakulima wadogo.

Naqshadaynta NidaamkaDesigning the Systemتصميم النظامKupanga Mfumo

Cadaadiska shaqada: 2–4 bar emitters-ka caadi. Masaafada sprinkler-ka: 60% radius (overlap fiican). Lateral pipe: PE/PVC 32–50 mm. Mainline: 50–75 mm. Filter waxa loo baahan yahay xitaa biyaha ceelaal ah.Operating pressure: 2–4 bar. Sprinkler spacing: 60% of throw radius (good overlap). Lateral pipe 32–50 mm; mainline 50–75 mm. Even well water needs a screen filter.ضغط التشغيل 2–4 بار. التباعد 60% من نصف القطر.Shinikizo 2–4 bar. Nafasi 60%.

Faa'iidooyinka iyo KhaladaadkaPros and Consالمزايا والعيوبFaida na Hasara

  • Faa'iido: baahid yar oo dhulka simaha, ku habboon dhul jiifka, gargaaraa frost protection.Pros: minimal land levelling, suits sloped fields, can protect against frost.المزايا — لا تسوية مكثفة.Faida — bila kusawazisha.
  • Khaladaad: qubasho biyo (10–25%) xilliga dabaysha iyo qorraxda kulul, oo kor u qaadaa cudurrada caleemaha.Cons: 10–25% water lost to wind and evaporation; wet leaves invite fungal disease.العيوب — تبخر وأمراض أوراق.Hasara — uvukizi na magonjwa.
  • Waqtiga ugu fiican: aroor hore (5–8 AM) ama habeenkii dambe (7–10 PM).Best timing: early morning (5–8 AM) or late evening (7–10 PM).التوقيت — صباح أو مساء.Wakati — asubuhi au jioni.

Hubi cadaadiska maalin walba.Setup cost: $1,200–$3,000/ha (excluding pump). Clean sprinkler heads every 2 months. Replace worn nozzles yearly. Check pressure daily.التكلفة 1,200–3,000$/هكتار. تنظيف وفحص دوري.Gharama $1,200–$3,000/hektari.
