Beerista Digaagga — Layers iyo BroilersPoultry Farming — Layers and Broilersتربية الدواجنUfugaji wa Kuku

2026-05-259 daqiiqo9 min read9 دقائقdakika 9

Beerista digaagga waa mid ka mida ganacsiyada ugu fudud ee xoolaha — caajis yar, dakhli dhakhso ah (45 maalmood broiler-ka, 5 bilood layer-ka). Hektarka kasta wax ka badan dheh dhul yar (50–200 m²) ayaa kaaga shaqayn 1,000 digaag. Poultry is one of the easiest livestock businesses — low capital, fast cash (broilers ready in 45 days, layers from 5 months). Just 50–200 m² can house 1,000 birds. الدواجن أسهل تجارة ماشية — استثمار قليل، عائد سريع. Kuku ni biashara rahisi yenye mapato ya haraka.

Cages: 4–6 layers cage kasta — wax-soosaar sare oo loo doortay ganacsiyaha waaweyn. Saqaf, dabaylo wanaagsan, iftin maalin kasta 16 saac (layers). Deep litter: 8–10 birds/m².
