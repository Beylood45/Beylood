Digniinta Ayaxa Juun 2026: Cabsi Cusub Geeska Afrika June 2026 Locust Alert: New Horn of Africa Threat تحذير الجراد يونيو 2026: تهديد جديد في القرن الأفريقي Tahadhari ya Nzige Juni 2026: Tishio Jipya la Pembe ya Afrika

2026-06-18 5 daqiiqo 5 min 5 دقائق dakika 5

FAO Locust Watch iyo DLCO-EA (Desert Locust Control Organization for Eastern Africa) ayaa Juun 13, 2026 ka soo saaray digniin ku saabsan soo noqoshada ayaxa cidlada (Desert Locust). Roobabkii Gu' 2026 ee aan caadiga ahayn ee ka da'ay waqooyiga Soomaaliya iyo Eritrea waxay abuureen xaalado ku habboon dhalitaanka ayaxa — kooxo yar-yari ayaa hadda laga arkay Bari iyo dhanka woqooyi-bari ee Soomaaliland. FAO Locust Watch and DLCO-EA (Desert Locust Control Organization for Eastern Africa) issued an alert on June 13, 2026 about a fresh Desert Locust resurgence. Unusual Gu 2026 rains over northern Somalia and Eritrea created ideal breeding conditions — small swarms have already been spotted in Bari and north-eastern Somaliland. أصدرت FAO Locust Watch وDLCO-EA في 13 يونيو 2026 تحذيرًا من عودة الجراد الصحراوي. الأمطار غير العادية في شمال الصومال وإريتريا خلقت ظروفًا مثالية للتكاثر. FAO Locust Watch na DLCO-EA walitoa tahadhari Juni 13, 2026 kuhusu kurudi kwa nzige wa jangwa. Mvua isiyo ya kawaida ya Gu 2026 ilisababisha hali nzuri ya kuzaliana.

Goosashada hore ka qaad: Hadii dalaggaagu (galley, masaggu, digir) uu hadda gaadhay 80% bislaynsho, goosashada hore ka qaad — ayaxu wuxuu cuni karaa beer dhameystiran 12 saacadood.Harvest early if possible: If your maize, sorghum, or cowpea is at 80% maturity, harvest early — a swarm can devour an entire field in 12 hours.احصد مبكرًا.Vuna mapema.

Saamayska Iqtisaadiga ah ee La Sii Filayo Expected economic impact الأثر الاقتصادي Athari za kiuchumi

Hadii la awoodi waayo in kooxaha hadda jira la xakameeyo gudaha 6 toddobaad, FAO waxay saadaalisay khasaare ku siman $45 million ee dalagga & daaqsinta Geeska Afrika xilliga Hagaa 2026. Soomaaliya kaligeeda waxay halis ugu jirtaa khasaare gaadha $12 million — galley, masaggu, iyo geedaha xoolaha dhuuxsada. Tani waxay ku dari kartaa qiimo-yare ka sokow $0.20 kg-ga galleyda dhammaadka 2026. If current bands aren't controlled within 6 weeks, FAO projects losses of $45 million in crops and pasture across the Horn during Hagaa 2026. Somalia alone faces up to $12 million in losses — maize, sorghum, and livestock forage. This could add ~$0.20/kg to maize prices by late 2026. قد تخسر المنطقة 45 مليون دولار إذا لم يُحتوَ الوضع خلال 6 أسابيع. Hasara ya $45 milioni ikiwa hawatadhibitiwa ndani ya wiki 6.
