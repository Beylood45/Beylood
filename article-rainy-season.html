Beerista Xilliga Roobka — Gu' iyo DeyrRainy Season Plantingالزراعة في موسم الأمطارKupanda Msimu wa Mvua

2026-05-186 daqiiqo6 min6 دقائقdakika 6

Geeska Afrika waxay leedahay laba xilli oo roob ah: Gu' (Maarso–Maajo) iyo Deyr (Oktoobar–Disembar). Beero badan oo Soomaaliyeed isku tiirsanaaya roobka, sidaa darteed maaraynta wakhtiga roobka ayaa ah sirta wax-soosaarka.The Horn of Africa has two rainy seasons: Gu (March–May) and Deyr (October–December). Most Somali farms are rain-fed, so timing with the rains is the heart of productivity.موسمان: غو وديير.Misimu miwili: Gu na Deyr.

Gu' (Maarso–Maajo)Gu (March–May)غوGu

Roob: 200–400 mm. Cimilo: kulul iyo qoyaan. Dalagga ugu fiican: galleyda, masaggada, cowpea, basasha, yaanyada. Beero 1–2 toddobaad ka dib roobka 30 mm ee ugu horeysa. Gurashada: Agoosto.Rain: 200–400 mm. Climate: hot and humid. Best crops: maize, sorghum, cowpea, onion, tomato. Plant 1–2 weeks after the first 30 mm rain. Harvest: August.200–400 ملم، حصاد أغسطس.200–400 mm, Agosti.

Deyr (Oktoobar–Disembar)Deyr (October–December)دييرDeyr

Roob: 150–250 mm (oo aan caadi ahayn). Cimilo: qabow oo qaboow. Dalagga ugu fiican: noocyo dhakhso ah (cowpea 60-day, masaggada 90-day, basbaaska). Gurashada: Janaayo–Febraayo.Rain: 150–250 mm (less reliable). Climate: cooler. Best crops: short-cycle (60-day cowpea, 90-day sorghum, pepper). Harvest: January–February.150–250 ملم.mm 150–250.

Hagayaal WakhtiTiming TipsالتوقيتVidokezo

  • Beero kahor inta lagu siiyo seeraha la xidha cudur (mildew, blight).Plant early to escape disease pressure that builds late.زراعة مبكرة.Panda mapema.
  • Hubi forecast 7-day ka hor inta aanad beerin.Check the 7-day forecast before sowing.راجع توقعات 7 أيام.Angalia siku 7.
  • Ku xidh masaggada iyo cowpea — masaggada cabir biyo, cowpea ku biiriyo N.Intercrop sorghum + cowpea — sorghum uses water, cowpea fixes N.زراعة مختلطة.Mseto.
  • Daboolaad iniinaha xilliga gu' — roob badan oo dhakhsada ah way burburin kara seedlings.Protect seedlings in heavy Gu rains — strong storms can wash them out.احمِ الشتلات.Linda miche.
