Tijaabinta Carrada — Hagaha BuuxaSoil Testing — The Complete Guideاختبار التربة — الدليل الشاملUpimaji wa Udongo — Mwongozo Kamili

2026-05-258 daqiiqo8 min read8 دقائقdakika 8

Beero badan ayaa bacrin ku ridda iyaga oo aan ogayn waxa ciidda u baahan tahay. Tijaabin saxan oo qiimaheedu yar yahay ($15–$40 muunad kasta) waxay kaa kaydin kartaa boqolaal doolar oo bacrin oo aan loo baahnayn iyo kor u qaadda wax-soosaarka 20–40%. Most farmers apply fertiliser without knowing what their soil actually lacks. A simple lab test ($15–$40 per sample) can save hundreds of dollars in wasted fertiliser and boost yields 20–40%. معظم المزارعين يضعون السماد دون معرفة ما تحتاجه التربة. اختبار بسيط ($15–$40 للعينة) يوفر مئات الدولارات ويرفع الإنتاج 20–40%. Wakulima wengi huweka mbolea bila kujua kile ambacho udongo unahitaji.

Goorma La TijaabiyoWhen to Testمتى تختبرLini Kupima

Marka labaad sannadkii (xilliga jiilaalka), kahor inta aanad bacrimin. Hadii aad bilaabayso dhul cusub, tijaab ka hor inta aanad beerin. Beero badan ayaa tijaaba 2–3 sannad kasta — laakiin pH iyo organic matter wuxuu si tartiib ah u beddelan. Every 2 years (during dry season), before applying fertiliser. Test new fields before planting. pH and organic matter change slowly. كل سنتين قبل الزراعة. الحقول الجديدة قبل البدء. Kila miaka 2, kabla ya mbolea.

Ka fogow xeero, jidkii xoolaha, ama wasakhda. 3. Isku dar muunadaha aagga, qalalo qorraxda, oo dir 500 g shaybaadhka. 4. Isticmaal weel nadiif ah; ha isticmaalin weel bacriminta. 1. Mix sub-samples, sun-dry, and send 500 g to the lab. 4. Use a clean container; never one that held fertiliser. 1. قسّم الحقل لمناطق. 2. 10–15 عينة فرعية على عمق 15–20 سم. 3. امزج، جفّف، أرسل 500 غرام للمختبر. 1. Gawanya shamba. 2. Tijaab Guryaha Loo SameeyoDIY Field Testsاختبارات منزليةVipimo vya Shambani

Texture jar test: Ku shub ciid 250 g jaag biyo ah, dhiq, oo daa 24 saac. Saddex layer ayaa kala soocda: ciid (sand), gallad (silt), iyo dhoobo (clay). pH test strips: $10–$15 oo ku siisa ujeedo. Worm count: 10 dirxiyo ciid 30 cm laba-jibaaran = ciid caafimaad qabta. Texture jar test: shake 250 g soil in a jar of water, let settle 24 hrs — three layers separate (sand, silt, clay). pH strips: $10–$15 give a rough reading. Worm count: 10 worms per 30 cm² = healthy soil. اختبار البرطمان: 250 غ تربة في ماء، ينفصل لرمل وغرين وطين. Mtihani wa chupa: changanya 250 g udongo na maji.

Sida Natiijada Loo IsticmaaloActing on Resultsاستخدام النتائجKutumia Matokeo

Beerta ka samee qorshe bacrin (fertiliser plan) ku saleysan natiijada iyo dalagga aad rabto inaad beerto. Tusaale: galleyda 5 t/ha = u baahnaa 100 kg N + 40 kg P + 40 kg K hektarkii. Hadii ciidda haysato 40 kg N + 20 kg P, ku dar oo keliya 60 kg N + 20 kg P. Tani waxay kaydsataa lacag iyo ka hortagaysaa wasakheynta biyaha. Build a fertiliser plan from the results and your target crop. Example: 5 t/ha maize needs 100 N + 40 P + 40 K. If soil already has 40 N + 20 P, apply only the gap: 60 N + 20 P. This saves money and prevents water pollution. اصنع خطة تسميد من النتائج ومحصولك. Tengeneza mpango wa mbolea kutoka matokeo.
