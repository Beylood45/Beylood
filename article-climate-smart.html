Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA)Climate-Smart Agricultureالزراعة الذكية مناخياًKilimo Stahimilivu

2026-05-217 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) waa qaab beerin ah oo isku biiriya: 1) Kor u qaadid wax-soosaarka, 2) Adkaysiga cimilada, 3) Yarayn gaaska keenta isbeddelka cimilada. Hay'adaha FAO iyo CGIAR ayaa ku abuuray.Climate-Smart Agriculture (CSA) integrates three goals: 1) raising productivity, 2) building climate resilience, 3) cutting greenhouse gases. Designed by FAO and CGIAR.CSA يدمج الإنتاج والمقاومة والبيئة.CSA huunganisha mambo matatu.

Saddexda TiirThe Three Pillarsالأركان الثلاثةNguzo Tatu

  • Productivity: noocyo cusub, drip irrigation, soil testing, precision farming.Productivity: improved varieties, drip, soil testing, precision tools.الإنتاجية.Uzalishaji.
  • Adaptation: drought-tolerant crops, agroforestry, rainwater harvesting.Adaptation: drought-tolerant crops, agroforestry, water harvesting.التكيف.Kubadilika.
  • Mitigation: no-till, cover crops, manure management, agroforestry sequester CO₂.Mitigation: no-till, cover crops, manure management, trees sequester CO₂.التخفيف.Kupunguza.

Tilmaamooyinka Beerta CSACSA Practices on the FarmالممارساتMbinu

Conservation Agriculture (no-till + cover crops + rotation), agroforestry, integrated pest management, integrated soil fertility (compost + targeted fertiliser), iyo livestock-crop integration. Iskudar 3+ qaab si dakhli kor ah oo adkaysi sare loo helo.Conservation Agriculture (no-till + cover + rotation), agroforestry, IPM, integrated soil fertility (compost + targeted fertiliser), livestock-crop integration. Combine 3+ practices for top results.الجمع بين 3 ممارسات.Changanya mbinu 3+.

Maaliyadda CSACSA FinanceالتمويلFedha

Green Climate Fund, World Bank CSA programmes, iyo carbon markets ($15–$60/ton CO₂ kaydsanay). Beerta 10 hektar oo agroforestry ah waxay soo saari karta $300–$1,500 dakhli carbon ah sannadkii.Green Climate Fund, World Bank CSA programmes, and carbon markets ($15–$60/ton CO₂ stored). A 10 ha agroforestry farm can earn $300–$1,500/year in carbon credits.تمويل عبر سوق الكربون.Soko la kaboni.
