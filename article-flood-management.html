Maaraynta DaadadkaFlood Managementإدارة الفيضاناتKudhibiti Mafuriko

2026-05-207 daqiiqo7 min7 دقائقdakika 7

Soomaaliya, daadadka wabiga Shabeelle iyo Jubba waxay ka mid yihiin halista ugu wayn — sannadkii 2023 waxay khaarjiyeen 200,000+ hektar beero. Maaraynta wanaagsan waxay yarayn kartaa khasaaraha 60–80%.In Somalia, Shabelle and Juba river floods are a major threat — in 2023 they damaged 200,000+ ha of farmland. Good management cuts losses 60–80%.فيضانات شبيلي وجوبا تهدد المزارع.Mafuriko ya Shabelle na Juba.

Ka HortaggaPreventionالوقايةKuzuia

  • Bunds iyo dykes — taaganto biyo 30–80 cm oo ku xeeran beerta.Bunds and dykes — 30–80 cm earth walls around fields.سدود.Vichuguu.
  • Drainage channels — kanaalo biyaha tuura, jaranjaro 0.5–1%.Drainage channels — divert water away, 0.5–1% slope.قنوات الصرف.Mifereji.
  • Raised beds — beerta sare ka kor u qaadid 30–50 cm.Raised beds — lift planting area 30–50 cm.أحواض مرتفعة.Vitanda vya juu.
  • Geedaha xeebta — Casuarina, Acacia tortilis ka sabaa nabaad-guurka.Riverbank trees — Casuarina, Acacia tortilis stabilise banks.أشجار الضفة.Miti ya ukingoni.

Saadaalinta DaadadkaFlood ForecastingالتنبؤUtabiri

SWALIM (UN) waxay siisaa saadaal daadad 3–7 maalmood Soomaaliya. Buug Beylood-ka, raac saadaalkan oo ka guuri xoolaha iyo aaladaha aagga sare. Hadii saadaalku weyn yahay (red alert), guro dalagga 24 saac gudahood.SWALIM provides 3–7 day flood forecasts for Somalia. Move livestock and equipment to high ground when alerts come. For red alerts, harvest crops within 24 hrs.SWALIM يتنبأ بالفيضانات.SWALIM hutoa utabiri.

Soo Celinta ka DibPost-Flood RecoveryالتعافيKupona

  • Sii daa biyaha kanaalo, iska saar silt-ka iniinaha agtooda.Drain standing water, remove silt from seedling zones.اصرف المياه.Ondoa maji.
  • Tijaab pH iyo cabbiraadda dhulka — daadadka waxay keenaan cusbo.Test pH and salinity — floods often deposit salt.اختبر pH والملوحة.Pima pH na chumvi.
  • Beero noocyo dhakhso ah (cowpea 60 maalmood) si aad u soo celiso dakhliga.Plant fast crops (cowpea 60 days) to recover income.ازرع محاصيل سريعة.Panda mazao haraka.
  • Iska ilaali cudurrada faafa biyaha — wash xoolaha iyo aaladaha.Watch for water-borne disease — wash tools and livestock.احذر الأمراض.Linda dhidi ya magonjwa.
