Mulching Ciidda — Daboolitaan CarradaSoil Mulching — Top-Layer Protectionالتغطية للتربةMatandazo ya Udongo

2026-05-165 daqiiqo5 min5 دقائقdakika 5

Lakab mulch oo 5–10 cm ah wuxuu yareeyaa qubasho biyaha 30–50%, ka hortagaa nabaad-guurka, joojiya cawska, oo si tartiib ah u bacrimisaa ciidda. Tan iyo isticmaalka magaca biyo waraabin ah, mulching waa qalab tababar yar oo qiimo weyn leh.A 5–10 cm mulch layer cuts evaporation 30–50%, prevents erosion, suppresses weeds, and slowly enriches the soil. After any irrigation upgrade, mulching is the biggest cheap win.طبقة 5–10 سم تقلل التبخر 30–50%.Tabaka 5–10 sm hupunguza uvukizi 30–50%.

Walxaha Ugu FiicanBest MaterialsالموادVifaa

  • Caws qalalan — bilaash beero badan; nool 6 bilood.Straw/dry grass — free on most farms; lasts 6 months.القش.Nyasi.
  • Banana leaves — milmaan dhakhso, qiimo organic.Banana leaves — decompose fast, organic value.أوراق الموز.Majani ya ndizi.
  • Compost — bacrin iyo daboolaad isku mid.Compost — fertiliser and mulch in one.الكمبوست.Mboji.
  • Wood chips — fiican geedaha miraha, nool 1–2 sano.Wood chips — great for fruit trees, lasts 1–2 years.قشور الخشب.Vipande vya mbao.
  • Black plastic — kheyr aad u sare wax-soosaarka khudaarta.Black plastic — top yields for vegetable production.بلاستيك أسود.Plastiki nyeusi.

KhaladaadkaPitfallsالأخطاءMakosa

  • Mulch lagu shubay ciid qalalan = qoyaan lumiyaa hadda. Ka hor inta aanad dabooli, waraabi.Mulching dry soil traps the dryness. Water first.اروِ قبل المولش.Mwagilia kabla.
  • Mulch oo agteeda dhirta wuxuu sababaa qudhanka.Mulch touching stems causes rot.لا تلامس الساق.Epuka kugusa bua.
  • Walxo cusub (sawdust, qalalan aan loo coriin) waxay ka qaadayaan N-ka ciidda. Ku dar urea yar.Fresh sawdust steals N — add a little urea.نشارة طازجة تستنزف N.Sawdust huleta upungufu wa N.
