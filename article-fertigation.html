Fertigation — Bacrinta iyo BiyahaFertigation — Fertiliser Through Waterالتغذية بالريFertigation

2026-05-186 daqiiqo6 min6 دقائقdakika 6

Fertigation waxay isku darsataa irrigation iyo fertilisation — bacrinta waxa lagu shubaa biyaha drip-ka, oo dhirtu si toos ah ayey u hesho. Wuxuu yareeyaa baahida bacrinta 30–50% oo wax-soosaarka u kordhaa 15–30%.Fertigation combines irrigation and fertilisation — fertiliser is dissolved in the drip water and delivered straight to the roots. Cuts fertiliser need 30–50% and lifts yields 15–30%.التغذية بالري تجمع الري والتسميد.Fertigation huunganisha umwagiliaji na mbolea.

Sida loo SameeyoHow It Worksكيف يعملInavyofanya Kazi

Bacrinta cusboonaysiisan (urea, NPK, potassium nitrate) waxa lagu milmiyaa weel weyn (haan A) iyo macdano (haan B; calcium iyo nitrate ku ilaali). Venturi injector ama dosing pump waxay si fiican u shubaan dhirta. Saxnaanta: EC 1.5–2.5 dS/m, pH 5.5–6.5.Water-soluble fertilisers (urea, NPK, potassium nitrate) are dissolved into stock tanks (Tank A for nutrients, Tank B for calcium/nitrate). A Venturi injector or dosing pump meters them into the drip line. Target EC 1.5–2.5 dS/m, pH 5.5–6.5.الأسمدة الذوابة في خزانات A وB.Mbolea katika matanki A na B.

Bacrinta Caanka ahCommon Fertilisersالأسمدة الشائعةMbolea

  • Urea (46-0-0) — N kheyr; qiimo yar.Urea (46-0-0) — cheap nitrogen.اليوريا.Urea.
  • MAP (12-61-0) — P sare, ku habboon bilowga koritaanka.MAP (12-61-0) — high P for early growth.MAP.MAP.
  • Potassium nitrate (13-0-46) — K sare iyo N, fiican xilliga miraha.Potassium nitrate (13-0-46) — high K + N, perfect for fruiting.نترات البوتاسيوم.Potassium nitrate.
  • Calcium nitrate — calcium iyo N, joojiya blossom-end rot.Calcium nitrate — calcium + N, prevents blossom-end rot.نترات الكالسيوم.Calcium nitrate.

Tilmaamo Muhiim ahImportant TipsنصائحVidokezo

  • Ku dar bacrinta wakhtiga waraabinta 15-da daqiiqo dhexe; ka hor & ka dib biyaha kaliya si tuubada loo nadiifiyo.Inject during the middle 15 min of irrigation; clean water before and after to flush lines.حقن في الـ15 دقيقة الوسطى.Ingiza katikati ya dakika 15.
  • Hubi EC iyo pH asbuuc kasta.Check EC and pH weekly.افحص EC وpH أسبوعياً.Kagua EC kila wiki.
  • Iska ilaali calcium + sulphate haan kasta — way isku tagaan oo dhiig fadhig samaystaan.Never mix calcium and sulphate in the same tank — they precipitate.لا تخلط الكالسيوم والكبريت.Usichanganye kalsiamu na salfati.
