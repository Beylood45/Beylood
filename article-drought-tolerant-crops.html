Dalagga Abaaraha ka AdkaystaDrought-Tolerant Cropsالمحاصيل المقاومة للجفافMazao Sugu ya Ukame

2026-05-269 daqiiqo9 min read9 دقائقdakika 9

Geeska Afrika, abaaraha ayaa kororsanaaya bilaha. Beerista dalag isku tiirsan biyaha aad u badan (galley, bariis) waxay ku riixayaan beeralayda halis. Dalagga abaaro ka adkaysta — sida masaggada, cowpea, iyo millet — waxay siisaa beero badan kalsoonida cuntada xitaa sannadda abaarta. In the Horn of Africa, droughts are getting longer. Relying only on water-thirsty crops (maize, rice) puts farmers at risk. Drought-tolerant crops — sorghum, cowpea, millet — provide food security even in dry years. في القرن الإفريقي، تطول فترات الجفاف. تنويع المحاصيل المقاومة (الذرة الرفيعة، اللوبيا، الدخن) يوفر الأمن الغذائي. Mazao sugu ya ukame hutoa usalama wa chakula.

1. Masaggada (Sorghum)1. Sorghum1. الذرة الرفيعة1. Mtama

Sorghum bicolor waa dalag aasaasi ah oo Soomaaliya. Wuxuu ku korta 400–600 mm roob, wuxuu adkaystaa heerkulka 40°C. Wax-soosaarka: 2–4 t/ha caadi, 5–7 t/ha hybrid. Noocyada: Gadam, Wad Ahmed, Kuyuma. Wuxuu siiyaa: cunto (sarreen, soor), quudin xoolaha, iyo cabitaan dabiici ah. Sorghum bicolor is a staple in Somalia. It thrives on 400–600 mm rainfall and tolerates 40°C. Yields: 2–4 t/ha (traditional), 5–7 t/ha (hybrid). Varieties: Gadam, Wad Ahmed, Kuyuma. Uses: food (flour, porridge), animal feed, and brewing. الذرة الرفيعة أساسية في الصومال. 400–600 ملم، 2–4 طن/هكتار. Mtama ni chakula kikuu. 400–600 mm, tani 2–4/hektari.

2. Cowpea (Digirta)2. Cowpea2. اللوبيا2. Kunde

Vigna unguiculata waxay leedahay protein 25%, oo waxay adkaysataa abaaraha iyo ciidda nafaqo-yari. Wax-soosaarka: 1–2 t/ha digirta + 3–5 t/ha cunto xoolaha. Noocyada: M66, K80, IT99K. Faa'iidooyinka: ku biiriyaa 50–80 kg N/ha (legume), 60–90 maalmood gudahood ayuu gurmadaa, oo wuxuu noqdaa cunto, quudin, iyo bacrin. Vigna unguiculata packs 25% protein and handles drought and poor soils. Yields: 1–2 t/ha grain + 3–5 t/ha fodder. Varieties: M66, K80, IT99K. It fixes 50–80 kg N/ha, matures in 60–90 days, and serves as food, fodder, and green manure. اللوبيا 25% بروتين، تتحمل الجفاف وتثبت 50–80 كغ N. Kunde 25% protini, hustahimili ukame.

3. Pearl Millet (Mashayda)3. Pearl Millet3. الدخن3. Ulezi

Pennisetum glaucum waxay ku kortaa 250–500 mm roob (dalagga ugu adkaysiga abaaraha). Wuxuu adkaystaa ciid carro ah oo qalalan. Wax-soosaarka: 1–2 t/ha. Cuntada: sarreen ay leedahay nafaqo sare (iron, zinc, calcium). Wuxuu si fiican ula shaqeeyaa cowpea ahaan intercrop. Pennisetum glaucum thrives on as little as 250–500 mm rainfall — the most drought-tolerant cereal. Handles sandy, dry soils. Yields: 1–2 t/ha. Flour is rich in iron, zinc, calcium. Pairs well as an intercrop with cowpea. الدخن 250–500 ملم — أكثر الحبوب مقاومة للجفاف. Ulezi mm 250–500 — sugu zaidi kwa ukame.

4. Drought-Tolerant Maize (Galley)4. Drought-Tolerant Maize4. الذرة المقاومة4. Mahindi Sugu

Galleyda caadiga ah way liidatay xilliga abaaraha, laakiin noocyada cusub ee CIMMYT (sida DroughtTEGO) waxay ku korin karaan 350 mm roob. Wax-soosaarka: 3–5 t/ha xilli caadi ah, 2–3 t/ha xilli abaaro ah. Cigaarka iyo wax-soosaarka waa 30% ka badan yahay galleyda caadiga ah xilliga abaaraha. Standard maize fails in drought, but new CIMMYT-bred varieties (e.g., DroughtTEGO) grow with 350 mm of rain. Yields: 3–5 t/ha in good years, 2–3 t/ha in drought years — 30% better than standard maize when water is short. الذرة العادية تفشل بالجفاف، لكن DroughtTEGO تنجح بـ350 ملم. DroughtTEGO mahindi hustahimili 350 mm.

5. Cassava (Mihogo)5. Cassava5. الكسافا5. Mihogo

Manihot esculenta waxay ku kortaa dhul aan bacrin ahayn, oo waxay xilliga abaaraha sii joogtaa 18 bilood iyada oo aan wax saamayn ah ku yeelan xididka. Wax-soosaarka: 10–25 t/ha. Wuxuu noqdaa cunto, sarreen, iyo soo saarid ethanol-ka. Manihot esculenta grows on poor soils and can stay in the ground 18 months without losing root quality during drought. Yields: 10–25 t/ha. Used for food, flour, and ethanol. الكسافا تنمو في تربة فقيرة وتبقى 18 شهراً. Mihogo kwenye udongo duni.

6. Pigeon Pea (Digirta Qaaduuda)6. Pigeon Pea6. حمص الحمام6. Mbaazi

Cajanus cajan waa geed-digir ah oo nool 3–5 sano. Xididada waaweyn ee dheer waxay ka soo gaadhsiisaan biyaha 2 mitir. Fix 40–60 kg N/ha. Wax-soosaarka: 1–2 t/ha digir, plus quudin xoolaha. Ku habboon intercrop galleyda iyo masaggada. Cajanus cajan is a perennial legume (3–5 years). Deep tap roots reach water at 2 m. Fixes 40–60 kg N/ha. Yields: 1–2 t/ha grain plus fodder. Ideal intercrop with maize and sorghum. حمص الحمام بقولي معمر، جذور حتى 2 م. Mbaazi mizizi mirefu hadi 2 m.

Istaraatijiyada Beerta Abaaraha ka BadbaadaBuilding a Drought-Resilient Farmبناء مزرعة مقاومة للجفافShamba Sugu kwa Ukame

  • Ha ku tiirsanaan hal dalag — kala-duwanaanta dalagga waxay yarayn karaa khasaaraha 50%.Don't rely on one crop — diversification can halve drought losses.لا تعتمد على محصول واحد.Usitegemee zao moja.
  • Beero intercrop: galley + cowpea (50% galley + 50% cowpea waxay siisaa wadarta wax-soosaar leh oo aaminaad sare ku jirta).Intercrop: maize + cowpea — 50/50 mix gives more total food and steadier yields.زراعة مختلطة: ذرة + لوبيا.Mseto: mahindi + kunde.
  • Isticmaal noocyada koritaan dhakhso ah (60–90 maalmood) si aad u badbaadiso roobka.Use short-cycle varieties (60–90 days) to outrun rainfall windows.أصناف قصيرة الدورة.Aina za muda mfupi.
  • Ku xidh kaydinta biyaha (zai pits, terraces) si dhirta uu helo biyo dheeraad ah.Pair with rainwater harvesting (zai pits, terraces) for an extra moisture buffer.اربط بحفر زاي والمصاطب.Unganisha na mashimo ya zai.
  • Kaydi 20% wax-soosaarka sannad fiican sida insurance abaarta — silos qalalan, iniinaha xafidan.Store 20% of good-year yields as drought insurance — dry silos, seed banks.خزّن 20% كاحتياطي للجفاف.Hifadhi 20% kama akiba.
